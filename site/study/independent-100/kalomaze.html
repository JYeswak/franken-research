Relevance to our work

4 of 5 lenses: evals, training, local-models, memory, research-method

Min P is a sampler we can set today on llama.cpp/MLX local models. Their current posts name two failure modes we must guard against: eval scores polluted by harness parser bugs, and agent memory files full of non-generalizing notes. Most current work lives inside Prime Intellect repos.

The score is one reviewer’s judgement of how much this public work bears on our own work. It is not a ranking of people and says nothing about the person or the quality of their work.

What we would study next

  1. Tool-call parser / chat-template confound thread (x.com/kalomaze/status/2101192284025069884). Before trusting any cross-model eval we run, check that the harness parses each model's tool calls correctly Effort: small.
  2. Narrow memory-note critique (x.com/kalomaze/status/2099006407953838541). Test our ee/cass/retain rules against it: do our memories state principles or instances? Effort: small.
  3. Min P PR and discussion (github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/3841). Default sampler choice for local MLX/llama.cpp models Effort: small.

Before reusing anything: Min P ships inside MIT llama.cpp. koboldcpp fork is AGPL-3.0; do not vendor. Note the min-p paper's authors do not include kalomaze.
