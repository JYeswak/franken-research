Franken Research · independent assessment · Sep 2026

FR · 44 AI-built projects. Every claim checked.

Jeffrey Emanuel’s FrankenSuite is 44 ambitious repos written mostly by AI coding agents. We froze each one at a single commit, read the code, and graded every claim: what runs, what is only promised, and what nobody can verify yet. The 3 worth a bounded trial on real work sit on the amber ring near the center.

Made by Joshua Nowak (ZestStream), independent of the maintainer.

Watched daily by a GitHub Action: what changed, open issues.

2 of 44 show CI (continuous integration) passing at the commit we pinned (the single assessed commit). Of the other 42, 11 fail there and the rest have CI disabled, missing, private, or silent.

The honesty machinery is real. Negative-evidence ledgers, claim registries, fail-closed gates: unusually well built, and mostly not running where it counts. What is worth stealing

38 of 44 carry an AI-lab license rider: a clause that bars OpenAI and Anthropic from any use, even testing and benchmarking.

All 44 have a bus factor of 1 (one maintainer, no backup): the number of people who can leave before a project stalls. Ours is 1 too.

How to read it
Invest (0): independent validation plus governance. Empty in this program.
Pilot (3): release artifact + a bounded, real workload fit. Not a recommendation to ship.
Explore (34): real code, unproven claims
Monitor (7): watch, don’t land
green glow = CI green at the pin: CI (continuous integration) builds passing on the assessed commit. The pin is the single commit the assessor fixed for the whole evaluation. frankenscipy and franken_threed, the only 2
The rider = MIT + OpenAI/Anthropic rider, barring the AI labs from training, use, or benchmarking. 38 of 44 carry it: the domed ones when you toggle the ceiling.
white cage = plain MIT, the suite’s only plain-MIT repo (1 of 6 without the rider)
red cage = no license file: all rights reserved, the most restricted state (5)
bus factor = how many people can leave before the project stalls. It is 1 across the suite: one maintainer, checked by no one.
Bigger node = higher TRL (2–8); height above the plane climbs with TRL too. A thin vertical band marks a TRL range, plotted as a span, never a midpoint. frankentui_website is a support asset, so it sits flat on the plane: N/A as technology. The 38 rider-bound repos show a dome with “Show the rider ceiling”, every dome the same size, by design.
No license file is not freer than plain MIT. It means no operative grant at all: more restricted than the rider, which still grants rights to everyone outside the AI labs.
Map exceptions, not the rule: frankentui_website is ~36k lines of TypeScript, the two beads repos are static dashboard exports, and franken_agent_detection is not a rewrite of an incumbent.

A judgment map, not a measurement. Every verdict is an analyst’s inference from a packet frozen at one commit (Sep 2026), confidence: medium. Size is readiness, not quality: a big Monitor node is still a website.

Data note: window.FRANKEN_DATA and its field schema

This page and the map above both read from one global, window.FRANKEN_DATA (loaded from assets/data.js). Aggregate counts on the map and table are computed from these records at render time.

FRANKEN_DATA.repos is an array of 44 records, one per repo:

name
Repo name. Full brief at briefs/<name>.html.
trl
TRL as a display string ("4–5"); support assets read "N/A".
trlLow / trlHigh
Numeric TRL bounds. Ranges are plotted and sorted as spans, never midpoints.
techNA
true when TRL is N/A as technology (a support asset, not a technology).
nodus
Verdict ring (NODUS, the method’s four assessment rings): Invest, Pilot, Explore, or Monitor.
license
License verdict in plain words.
licenseKey
rider (MIT + AI-lab rider), mit (plain MIT), none (no license file).
ci
CI (continuous integration) state at the assessed commit, in plain words.
ciKey
C1: CI green at the pin. C2: CI red at the pin. C3: CI exists, no pin verdict. C4: no test CI (deploy-only at most). C5: CI disabled or deleted. C6: tests only on maintainer-private machines.
release
Release-artifact status at the pin.
blurb
The one-paragraph verdict.

FRANKEN_DATA.licenseClasses labels and explains the three license states. FRANKEN_DATA.ringDefinitions glosses the four rings. FRANKEN_DATA.visualEncoding pins rendering parameters such as the rider-dome radius.
