## 3. Honesty guardrails (expanded)

**Claim matrix (§20.4, "Witness: the claim-to-proof matrix")** [Verified]: "Every public claim — compatibility, soundness, determinism, performance, recovery, security — is a row: claim id · exact text or template · allowed evidence state (`OBSERVED` measured with a no-mock reproduction command | `TARGETED` gate specified, proof incomplete | `HYPOTHESIS` research annex | `PROVEN` a formal result with stated scope | `BLOCKED` | `RETIRED`) · owner workstream · scope (epoch/platform/mode/corpus) · required gate and artifact schemas · freshness predicate · forbidden stronger wording · fallback text. Documentation CI parses the docs against the matrix and rejects wording stronger than the evidence permits; coverage counts are generated from the ledgers, never hand-maintained. A benchmark is `OBSERVED`, never `PROVEN`; a Crucible-checked theorem is `PROVEN` relative to the assumptions its receipt names."

**Claim types D7** [Verified]: every load-bearing statement is exactly one of `invariant` (FL-INV-*), `proof`, `bounded_model`, `statistical`, `slo`, `benchmark`. "A weaker class may inform policy but may never enforce or justify a stronger one." Plus determinism classes D0–D4 carried on cache keys, receipts, and the Parity Ledger.

**Negative-evidence ledger** (Frontier Protocol §4, "Negative evidence as a first-class asset") [Verified]: "A failed attempt is valuable only when it makes repetition harder." Each row: immutable attempt id; precise hypothesis; exact observation that falsified it; changed and protected surfaces; revert status; the differentiator a future attempt must state. Rule: "**An agent MUST NOT repeat a recorded failed hypothesis unless its capsule names a concrete differentiator. 'Try again more carefully' is not a differentiator.**" Valid differentiators: "a new pinned artifact or decoded telescope; a corrected binder scope; a narrower change surface; a new counterexample or refusal cell; a different scheduler seed exposing a distinct causal path." Lifecycle position: the protocol is explicitly "proposed constitutional design extension; implemented read-only selection contract in §10.4" — so the negative-evidence schema is *proposed* with selection machinery partially live, not yet fully enforced. The frontier-view ordering (§10.2) already includes "top negative-evidence differentiators" and "closure evidence still missing" ahead of "exact next command" — minimizing context load while keeping failures drill-downable.

**Auto-demotion** [Verified], installed in three places: (a) shadow-run promotion (§12.6/§18.9): "promotion per workload/epoch/platform cell with deterministic sampling continuing afterward and auto-demotion on any soundness, fidelity, or tail-latency breach — promotion is a matrix, never a global flag"; (b) safe mode (§20.4): "a promoted lane that breaches its thresholds demotes itself and files the incident bundle"; (c) perturbation (§10.6): "a failing perturbation demotes the producer to `Opaque` … and opens a Parity-Ledger bead"; (d) risk register R15: "demote the affected surface/platform to R3 until the row is classified and fixtured." Demotion is machine-executed and incident-bundled, never silent.

**"What this does not earn" discipline** [Verified]: a recurring AGENTS.md prose pattern where every mechanism states its own limits — e.g. after the `verify && commit` rule: "**What this does not earn.** Still a rule and not a mechanism: nothing runs the diff, and making it gate is a shell habit rather than a guard." One host, one commit, class `bounded_model`. Claims about claims are required to say what class of evidence they are.

**Inconclusive-is-not-rejected (FL-INV-07)** [Verified]: "Resource exhaustion, cancellation, and internal faults yield typed `Inconclusive`/`InternalFault` outcomes, never rendered as, cached as, or promoted to acceptance *or* rejection."

**Pessimistic projections are as forbidden as optimistic ones** [Verified] (AGENTS.md): "**A pessimistic projection from one sample is as forbidden as an optimistic one.**" Motivation from measurement: at 10:00Z a swarm-wide freeze was called on 1.7G free, recovered unaided in four minutes — "the freeze was false, and it stopped six panes" — while a perturbation measurement actually needed 512K against 64G free.

## 4. Plan→agent execution (task graphs, phases, verification loops, dialectical review, drift prevention)

**Task graph = bead graph, gated on evidence** [Verified]. 32 epics track the 12 workstreams (W1–W12); tasks/bugs hang beneath; blocking relations are explicit: "closure on cited evidence with blocker beads gated on their named probe, `br dep cycles` stays empty" (Work-Graph Discipline, AGENTS.md). Filing a bead costs three artifacts in one commit (bead + regenerated ownership projection + coverage row); the coverage row for an open bead is "legitimately sparse: every evidence array empty, the notes carrying the measurement and what it does not establish, because no repair is being claimed yet."

**Phases.** The workstreams are "sequenced by dependency rather than by scope reduction" (plan §1, [Maintainer claim]); the defining sequencing property: "the ABI/olean/kernel spine comes first," because G1 (Independent Judge) is "both the earliest shippable product and the deepest de-risking event the program can buy." No MVP anywhere: "Every subsystem is specified at full strength. Sequencing is by dependency, not scope reduction."

**Frontier selection** (Protocol §5) [Verified]: robot ranking = priority + critical-path descendants + blocked acceptance criteria released + context reuse + seam isolation − ownership/collision risk − evidence cost − trusted-surface breadth − irreducible uncertainty. Hard filters precede scoring: (1) dependencies closed or waived; (2) no live ownership conflict; (3) acceptance criterion is falsifiable; (4) a first failing boundary or discovery experiment is named; (5) required artifacts/toolchain available; (6) no Oracle-Only-Law or dependency-doctrine violation. `scripts/frontier_select.py` implements it; the handoff imports it ("The handoff reuses `scripts/frontier_select.py` rather than reimplementing task ranking").

**One-variable experiments** (Protocol §6) [Verified]: each implementation cycle states observation, one hypothesis, smallest intervention, protected surfaces, positive cell, negative cell, measurement, promotion rule. "Broad refactors are separate beads unless the current acceptance criterion requires them. A local seed mismatch does not authorize generic inference surgery; a synthetic green does not authorize a compatibility claim."

**Ownership and collision control** (Protocol §9) [Verified]: ownership is declared over *semantic seams*, not files ("fln-checker / Init.HEq expected recursor reconstruction"). Pre-commit revalidation (7 steps): refresh `main`, compare tracked blob hashes, inspect intervening commits on the seam, rerun the bounded experiment, run the local gate envelope, update the capsule with tested commit/tree, commit one coherent claim. "A stale but clean patch is not safe merely because Git applies it." Commits follow evidence claims (instrumentation → fix → real-artifact advancement → tracker refresh → design protocol); "Do not combine independent claims to reduce commit count."

**Drift prevention.** Four layers: (1) the Frontier Capsule binds every `in_progress` bead to an immutable anchor (branch/commit/tree/tracked blob hashes) plus lease and freshness grading current/reusable/stale/invalid; (2) deterministic handoffs — `scripts/agent_handoff.py snapshot --strict` is byte-deterministic, verified against reconstruction ("Verification uses the current verifier selector; a selector/schema change may therefore reject an older handoff. Regenerate… rather than relabeling an old payload as current"); (3) "Landing the Plane" 5-step session completion (file beads for remaining work, run gates, update statuses, flush+commit beads by path, hand off with changed/gates-results/risks/next-steps); (4) the publication-vs-notification doctrine: "landing a rule in this file reaches every *future* pane and no *current* one, because a pane already running has read this file and has no reason to read it again… **When you land a durable rule, say so to whoever sequences the swarm and ask for it to be broadcast, and record in your handoff which of the two you did.**"

**Dialectical review** [Inference from multiple verified artifacts]: no explicit "two LLM models grade each other" spec was found in this repo. The dialectic is institutionalized differently — as adversarial *mechanisms*, not model pairings: (i) dual kernel engines K1/K2 cross-checked with "disagreement halts, never outvotes"; (ii) council consensus with mandatory certified-engine seat — "there is no quorum that can vote a wrong judgment through"; (iii) the Tribunal's differential us-vs-Reference-oracle rigs; (iv) shadow-run dual lanes promoted per cell; (v) multi-pane adversarial measurement recorded in AGENTS.md's incident log (panes measure each other's rules, falsify the maintainer's overbroad prohibitions — "corrected here by its own author"; a pane's `NameError` from piping through `head` is disclosed against its own rule). The recurring primitive is *disagreement halts and is recorded*, never outvoted or smoothed over.

**Swarm operating reality** [Verified]: agents run as named panes (cc_1/2/3, cod_1/2/3, FoggyForge…) in one shared checkout with 1 author identity ("one author identity, one committer identity, one Co-Authored-By trailer — every field git offers is constant"), so "Identify your own commits by subject line, never by author" and name the sha in the next message. MCP Agent Mail was measured unreliable (four failures on 2026-07-27); the routing store (file drops at a shared path) plus broadcast-by-sequencer is the channel in use — with the documented hole that reading by address defeats itself, hence "read what is RECENT — never what is ADDRESSED."

## 6. Anti-satisficing (red-team / falsification / campaign mechanisms)

- **Mutation campaigns** (AGENTS.md) [Verified]: "Seeded defects (skipped positivity check, inverted universe condition, leaked transaction assignment, dropped retain, stale cache hit accepted) must each be *killed* by a named test; a surviving critical mutant blocks the gate." Applied forensically too: five mutants planted against the boundary membrane, "each dies at a **different** cell, including one that reddens the *green* control" — the control is itself tested.
- **Perturbation validation** (plan §10.6, §13.2) [Verified]: "mutate an allegedly-unread input and require the product unchanged — a failing perturbation demotes the producer to `Opaque`." Conservative-first, then ratchet on evidence.
- **No-mock lanes** (AGENTS.md) [Verified]: "Release-level claims close only against the real thing: real Reference binaries, real filesystems, real editor clients, real corruption. Mocked boundaries are fine for unit tests and rejected by the evidence gate."
- **First-failure monotonicity** (Protocol §8) [Verified]: "Only 'advanced' is progress on a sequential conformance bead. A larger passing test count with the same first failure is useful supporting evidence but not frontier advancement." Progress is defined as frontier movement, never test-count inflation.
- **Red-team by name**: no document titled red-team exists [Absent]; the function is distributed across mutation campaigns, fault drills (§19.2 "fault drills" as continuous obligation), thread-matrix per-commit determinism, the PG-5 waiver's adversarial self-correction, and the 12 named forbidden reward-hacking patterns ("gate self-weakening… proof-class inflation, golden regeneration reflex, commit-stream pumping, tautological tests, easy-lever cherry-picking, close-pump abuse, scope-splitting, spec-editing as progress, conformance metastasis, dependency smuggling, bench-path hardcoding" — listed in franken_lean's AGENTS.md Rule 0.5; definitions live in the suite-wide `/data/projects/AGENTS.md`, external to this repo).
- **Anti-optimism rules**: "reporting a loss is a success — one line, revert, next lever, no retraction narrative" [Verified]; pessimistic single-sample projections equally forbidden; claim classes forbid weaker evidence enforcing stronger claims.
- **Falsification posture**: the risk register's mitigations are stated with teeth ("freeze additive work and concentrate the program on the earliest failing convergence gate — stated here so invoking it is procedure, not crisis"), and the PG-5 waiver episode is the repo's own falsification case study: the maintainer documented where his waiver rested on a premise a later workflow disproved, named it "this session's dominant defect, a claim whose producer was never re-checked," and narrowed the waiver to the surviving reason.

## 8. Maturity verdict

**Mature.** [Inference, grounded in the verified artifacts above.] FrankenLean's planning apparatus is the most elaborated in the observed suite pattern: a 733-line constitutional plan with load-bearing-unknown retirement before interface freeze (G0's ten spikes), typed convergence gates (G1–G6), machine-checked performance gates (PG-1…PG-M), an evidence-state claim matrix enforced by documentation CI, decision records D-01–D-23, and a risk register with procedural tripwires; a 432-line frontier protocol with a typed machine-parseable capsule schema, negative-evidence ledger, evidence envelopes S0–S5, and a read-only robot selector; a 1,333-line AGENTS.md written as a dated measurement log in which agents falsify each other's rules; a 483-bead tracker with timestamp-bound closure judgements; and failure-positive norms (report a loss as success; pessimistic single-sample projections forbidden). The distinctive move is that planning here is not a roadmap — it is an **adversarial epistemic system**: every mechanism carries its own stated limits ("What this does not earn"), every waiver must be public and mechanically expiring, every gate bypass names its price, and disagreement is engineered to halt rather than be outvoted. Absences are naming-level, not functional — the missing `ROADMAP.md`/`docs/research/` functions exist under other names or as executable apparatus.
