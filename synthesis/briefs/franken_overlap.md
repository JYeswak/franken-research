## 3. CI/EVIDENCE BAR — what franken_overlap demonstrates that others lack

These are mechanisms present in franken_overlap's packet with **no equivalent found in any of the 43 sibling packets** (grep-verified: zero sibling hits for "paired-bootstrap," "incomplete, not extrapolated," "ci.yml.disabled," or natural-label adjudication machinery).

1. **`fo-claim-gate`: preregistered claim manifests with paired-bootstrap three-way verdicts.** What it does: benchmark claims are preregistered in a claim manifest; per-query paired-bootstrap confidence intervals adjudicate each claim to `supported` / `inconclusive` / `unsupported`, explicitly distinguishing "global candidate-stream gains from broadly distributed per-query gains" (packet claim 5, §4.5) [Code-verified, High on existence]. Who lacks it: all 43. The closest analogs are weaker or different in kind — franken_engine has "a working claim gate," but it is a documentation matrix that *rejects fixture evidence* ("the claim gate ate them"), not a statistical verdict machine; frankensympy has "machine-readable claim gates" tied to proof certificates; franken_markdown wires claims to proof scripts via `claims.tsv` but produces no verdicts; franken_lean's claim matrix has evidence states and doc CI, not paired bootstrap. Sibling packets *do* use bootstrap CIs on benchmark ratios (frankenredis per-row bootstrap 95% median CIs; frankenlibc's bootstrap median-CI/null-control gate; frankenpandas's bootstrap median-CI certification gate; franken_node's 95% CI via 10k bootstrap resamples) — but none preregisters claims and returns three-way verdicts from paired per-query tests.
2. **`fo-adjudicate`: natural-label adjudication.** What it does: human-adjudicated gold labels via a review workbench (`fo-bench` + `docs/GOLD_ADJUDICATION.md`) feeding claim verdicts [Code-verified, High on existence] (packet claim 5). Who lacks it: all 43 — sibling "adjudication" mentions are unrelated (franken_drone's epipolar/relative-pose adjudication of geometric candidates; frankenlibc's deletion-excess adjudication against a ledger; frankenpandas's intent adjudication).
3. **The "incomplete, not extrapolated" rule for over-budget baselines.** What it does: when a baseline exceeds its compute budget, the result is recorded as `incomplete` rather than extrapolated — a named anti-extrapolation policy for benchmark fairness [Maintainer claim, High] (packet claim 5, §4.5). Who lacks it: all 43 (zero sibling mentions). frankenpandas converges on the *practice* ("rows failing the gate … are counted, not averaged" [Code-verified, High]) but has no named policy.
4. **Uncertainty receipts as first-class evidence-bundle contents.** What it does: the immutable Markdown/HTML proof bundles pin corpus, query, commit, compiler, hardware, baseline, quality, span, latency, *and uncertainty receipts* as required bundle contents per `docs/EMPIRICAL_STATUS.md` [Maintainer claim, High] (packet deepening Q4). Who lacks it: all 43. Bundles/attestations exist elsewhere (frankenterm's sigstore-signed attestation bundles [Verified]; franken_engine's signed evidence ledger; frankenredis's "proof-bundle culture"; franken_snowflake's BLAKE3 content-addressed receipts) — but none names uncertainty receipts as a required schema element.
5. **Deliberately disabled CI as a checked-in, documented governance artifact.** What it does: `ci/github-actions-disabled/ci.yml.disabled` states the rationale verbatim — "Disabled intentionally: this repository is validated on owner-controlled machines" — making the CI absence a checkable decision rather than decay [Code-verified, High] (packet claim 7). Who lacks it: all 43 — zero sibling packets contain the literal artifact. (Outcome is shared per Pattern B above; the *documentation* of the choice is not.)
6. **Fail-closed `.foidx` v2 index parsing with a conformance corruption-rejection test.** What it does: malformed magic, unknown versions/flags, unsorted dictionaries, invalid postings, inconsistent doc frequencies, impossible sizes, and trailing bytes are rejected; the trailing-bytes case has a conformance test (packet claim 4; VALIDATION.md) [Code-verified, High]. Who lacks the exact form: all 43 — fail-closed *semantics* exist elsewhere (frankenscipy's "strict/hardened mode split with fail-closed semantics"; frankenjax's "pmap collectives fail closed"; frankengit's lanes that "refuse with exit 3 instead of returning a false green") but none applies it to an index/container format with a corruption-rejection contract test.

Also-does-X (explicitly not unique): the zero-`unsafe` workspace-forbid posture is shared with 32 sibling packets; the negative-evidence/honesty-discipline culture is shared with frankenscipy, franken_node, franken_whisper, frankenfs, frankenterm, franken_networkx, frankenpandas, and others.
