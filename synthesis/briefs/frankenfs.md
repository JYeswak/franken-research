## 1. OWN-PACKET DISTILLATION

- **Verdict:** "genuinely real, mountable, clean-room Rust reimplementation of ext4 and btrfs" [Inference, High] — not vaporware: "a fresh shallow clone of HEAD confirms 22 workspace members and 663,710 lines of first-party Rust [Verified, High]"; "every crate root carries `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` backed by a workspace-level `unsafe_code = "forbid"` lint [Verified, High]"; "the vendored `fuser` 0.17.0 transport (ABI 7.42) is explicitly outside the unsafe ban and contains real unsafe code, which the README states rather than hides [Verified, High]".
- **TRL:** 4 (aggregate: ext4 read/inspect/FUSE-read 5–6; ext4 RW 4; btrfs RW 3).
- **NODUS ring:** Explore. The packet is explicit about the ring-down logic: a tagged release (v0.2.0) exists and a bounded workload (forensic inspection, FS experiment lab) is conceivable, "but HEAD's main CI workflow is failing, the maintainer's own readiness lanes are 'mid-evidence,' there is zero independent validation, and the no-contributions policy means no institution can adopt-and-improve. When in doubt, ring down."
- **Strongest strength:** the self-falsifying evidence discipline. The packet's own words: "the May-2026 reality check found its own btrfs read-write was a *silent-data-loss facade* and published it; the August-2026 worker-scope audit admitted 166 published benchmark rows cannot prove which machine they ran on." The negative-evidence ledger is 20,556 lines [Verified, High]; the mounted scorecards "open with all-caps anti-hype warnings, including one that proves the project's best btrfs 'win' was a transport illusion."
- **Strongest ceiling:** the governance compound — "bus factor 1 with an explicit no-contributions policy, CI red on the main workflow at HEAD, and a non-OSI rider license — which caps adoption, contribution, and independent validation all at once."

**The 3 most important pieces of evidence (with tiers):**

1. **The worker-scope re-scoping (Aug 2026).** The maintainer measured "the *same cell* at `1.2693x` on one `rch` worker and `0.0093x` on another — a **13.6x swing with both A/A nulls passing** — because an A/A null controls within-invocation noise only and is blind to between-worker CPU, cache, and memory-bandwidth differences. The ledger now enforces a ratchet: that count of unattributed rows may only fall, and new rows must record their host." [Maintainer claim, Medium — the audit is the maintainer's own, but the confession is against interest; the ratchet mechanism is described in the tree — Verified, High.] This is the packet's single most load-bearing finding: it falsifies the control the rest of the suite still trusts (see §2, §5).
2. **The transport-confound analysis.** The btrfs fsync "win" (0.449–0.463x, "2.2x faster," admitted twice, ELF `c9fb745f…`) flips to **1.491904x — 1.49x *slower*** — when both arms run on a loop device (`--fuse-transport loop`): "A 3.2x swing from transport alone… Do not quote them as filesystem wins" [Verified from the project's own docs, High]. The scorecard's doctrine: a row that does not name its host "is valid *on an unrecorded machine*, never as comparable to a row measured elsewhere."
3. **The silent-data-loss facade finding (May-2026, repaired through Aug 2026).** "May-2026 reality check found btrfs RW was a **silent-data-loss facade** (mutations against an in-memory CoW tree, `outcome="applied"` logged, nothing persisted)" [Maintainer-admitted, High]; the August commits document root causes at commit granularity — "`839eb708`: the durable commit could not serialize its own leaves, so fsync returned EINVAL and nothing persisted above ~18 MiB/transaction" and "`241093de` and `9d64f4a1`: a write failing with ENOSPC destroyed the data it could not replace" (`docs/MOUNTED_BTRFS_SCORECARD.md`). The pinned HEAD commit's own message — `fix(repair): serialize and durably publish ownership lease mutations` — is further evidence: "at HEAD, the project is still fixing *durability of the repair subsystem's own mutations*. The durability story is being written in the present tense, not the past. [Verified from git log, High]"

## 3. CI/EVIDENCE BAR — what frankenfs demonstrates that other packets lack

**3.1 The Artifact Gates second CI lane.** FrankenFS runs a second workflow at HEAD that passes while the main lane is red: "`Artifact Gates` workflow: completed, success on the same push" where the main `CI` workflow is "completed, failure" [CI-observed via GitHub Actions API, High]. A full-suite grep for "artifact[- ]gate" matches **only** frankenfs-assessment.md — no other packet names or describes such a lane. Who lacks it: everyone else, most pointedly **franken_agent_detection** (single lane that "dies at `cargo fmt` before a single one of the 1,226 tests executes," so fmt-strangulation kills all evidence) and **asupersync** ("zero workflow runs exist at the pinned commit," all behavioral claims resting on unobservable maintainer-run DSR gates). FrankenFS's Artifact Gates — which carry the crash/replay artifact gates and the 12-scenario gate inventory — keep *evidence* flowing independent of the *lint* lane. No other packet demonstrates this decoupling.

**3.2 The worker-scope preflight ratchet.** A full-suite grep for "worker[- ]scope" matches only frankenfs-assessment.md. The mechanism: after measuring a 13.6× same-cell swing across `rch` workers with both A/A nulls passing, the ledger "enforces a ratchet: that count of unattributed rows may only fall, and new rows must record their host." [Maintainer claim, Medium / tree-described Verified, High]. Twelve other packets quote A/A nulls (frankenredis, franken_numpy, frankenpandas, franken_networkx, frankenscipy, franken_whisper, frankenmermaid, franken_snowflake, franken_node, frankenjax, frankenlibc, frankensympy) — none of them has a host-binding ratchet, and none of them has published the finding that invalidates the control they use.

**3.3 The canonical-gate execution binding.** On 2026-09-11, "seven gate commands [were] bound to the execution-evidence path… but all seven report `not_implemented` (no gate tests exist in any owning crate yet); the binding itself is pinned by `canonical_gates::tests::catalog_commands_equal_the_spec_document`" [Verified, High], and "a cargo filter selecting zero tests is classified `not_implemented`, *never a pass*." What others lack: the *binding mechanism*. **franken_alignment** shares the bare `not_implemented` convention (roadmap gates G1–G6 are `not_implemented` in `registry/roadmap.json` [Code-verified, High]) but as *status entries*, not fail-closed execution bindings. **franken_tts** shares the *name* ("canonical gate") for something different: a determinism env-var (`FTTS_CANONICAL_NORM=1`) under which "browser PCM matches the CLI sample-for-sample (96,000/96,000, max 0 LSB)." No other packet binds gate commands to execution evidence such that an empty selection reports itself as unimplemented.

**3.4 The transport-confound isolation analysis in mounted scorecards.** Full-suite grep for "transport[- ]confound|transport illusion|transport arm" matches only frankenfs-assessment.md. The mechanism: symmetric-transport bench arms (the comparator's `--fuse-transport loop` mode), both-arms-on-loop re-measurement that sign-flipped the headline btrfs row from a 0.449–0.463x "win" to a 1.491904x loss, sign-changed rows, and all-caps anti-hype warnings in the scorecards. **franken_networkx** has "Gauntlet + cc release scorecards" with '"DOMINATES" verdicts' — scorecards *without* the confound analysis; **frankenscipy** and **frankentorch** publish "scorecards" too. None documents a transport-confound isolation. This is the evidence practice no other packet has.

**3.5 Crash/replay artifact gates + the epoch FSM.** "The epoch state machine (`staged ≥ visible ≥ durable`) with six formal invariants (I1–I6) and a 12-scenario crash/replay artifact gate [README + code-structure, Medium on runtime behavior — not executed by analyst]." Durability-adjacent packets (**frankensqlite**, **frankenredis** with its persistence claims, **frankengraphdb**) show no equivalent artifact-gated crash-consistency evidence in their packets.

**3.6 A published, red sanitizer scan.** "ubs scan (2026-09-21 reality check): 498 files scanned, **254 critical findings** (Sept-8 baseline: 244), 17,559 info, runner `partial` — 'No clean scan claimed; criticals include sampled false positives'" [Maintainer claim, Medium]. ## 4. SHARED HURDLES — structural, with evidence

The frankenfs packet names the consequence: "review depth is already the binding constraint — at this commit rate, one human cannot review 663,710 lines, so correctness already rests on the harness, not on review."

**4.2 The non-OSI license rider.** FrankenFS: MIT **"with an 'ADDITIONAL RIDER / RESTRICTION (OpenAI / Anthropic)'"** — "'Notwithstanding any other provision of this License, no rights are granted to any Restricted Party'" — "**NOT OSI open source** [Verified, High]", with "use" defined to include "benchmarking, testing, analyzing, indexing." **4.3 Evidence rot between executed gates.** FrankenFS: "integrity manifests drift silently because nothing runs the pins at commit time; a single JSON-formatting commit broke six harness targets" [Verified/CI-observed, High]. **4.4 The pinned-nightly aging trap.** FrankenFS pins `nightly-2026-08-31` (rustc 1.99), with the toolchain file documenting exactly why ("a 2026-07-21 lint promotion broke the build and rustup once swapped the toolchain mid-build") [Verified, High]. ## 5. GENUINE UNIQUENESS — things no other packet does

Method: full-suite greps for the distinguishing terms; "unique" is claimed only where the grep matched frankenfs-assessment.md alone. Honest "also does X" callouts are given inline.

**5.1 The worker-scope preflight ratchet.** Unique (grep "worker[- ]scope" → only frankenfs). Twelve sibling packets trust A/A nulls; frankenfs is the only one to *falsify the control itself* and turn the falsification into a ledger-enforced ratchet.

**5.2 The transport-confound isolation protocol.** Unique (grep "transport[- ]confound|transport illusion|transport arm" → only frankenfs). franken_networkx/frankenscipy/frankentorch publish "scorecards" without any confound-isolation; only frankenfs's scorecard contains a proved sign-flip (0.449–0.463x → 1.491904x) plus the symmetric-arm machinery.

**5.3 The Artifact Gates second CI lane.** Unique (grep "artifact[- ]gate" → only frankenfs). The "also does X" nuance: single-lane CI and zero-workflow-runs-at-pin are the suite norm (asupersync, franken_agent_detection); nobody else runs a *second* lane that decouples evidence continuity from the lint lane.

**5.4 Block-level MVCC with the merge-proof taxonomy and expected-loss arbitration.** Unique in substance (grep "merge[- ]proof" → only frankenfs): "`MergeProofMechanism::{NoSameBlockMerge, AppendOnly, RangeOverlay, BitmapOr, BitmapDelta}` and `ConflictPolicy::{Strict, SafeMerge(default), Adaptive}` verified in code [Verified, High]"; `ffs-mvcc` is 32,831 lines. "Also does X" callouts: **franken_alignment** has "epistemic MVCC" — MVCC *for judgments* in an agent-control plane (FA-058/FA-059), a different animal in a different domain; **frankengraphdb** unifies "MVCC/time-travel/git-style branches/replication" only as an *unbuilt medium bet* ("One Version Universe — 'Chronicle'"). **5.5 A shipped, hand-rolled RFC 6330 fountain codec.** `ffs-repair` (31,572 lines): "no `raptorq` crate in any workspace manifest; the codec is first-party (`symbol.rs`, `codec.rs`, `lrc.rs`)" [Verified, High]. "Also does X" callouts: **frankengraphdb** has a fountain-coded commit stream only as a medium bet (B1); **frankensqlite** invokes fountain-coding solely as a bear-case punchline ("nobody's SQLite corruption story ends with 'if only my WAL frames were fountain-coded'"). FrankenFS is the only project in the suite with a *shipped* fountain-code subsystem.

**5.6 The mountable clean-room ext4+btrfs filesystem in safe Rust.** Domain-unique: no other packet among the 44 is a filesystem. The distinctive mechanisms — JBD2 journal attachment on the ext4 read-write CLI mount path, the ~1,866-line `btrfs_full_transaction_commit`, the vendored-`fuser` (ABI 7.42) mount path — have no counterpart anywhere else in the suite.

## 6. CROSS-POLLINATION IN — other packets' methods frankenfs should adopt

**6.2 Registry-invariant tests that fail the build on drift → from franken_agent_detection → why it fits frankenfs → expected payoff.**

**6.3 CI-blocking baseline gate with per-row environment tagging and no skip waiver → from asupersync → why it fits frankenfs → expected payoff.**

**6.5 Machine-checkable `host=`/`worker=`/`harness=`/ELF-hash row contract → from franken_numpy → why it fits frankenfs → expected payoff.**
franken_numpy: "machine-checkable `host=`/`worker=`/`harness=`/ELF-hash row contract" — its packet judges it "directly importable into how the program evaluates the other 42 repositories." These are the bench-heavy packets quoting ratios with A/A null controls — frankenredis's table carries "an in-invocation A/A null control on every row (nulls 0.979–0.999)" [Maintainer claim, Medium] — or, in franken_snowflake's case, rows with "no A/A null, no hardware pinning" at all. FrankenFS's finding applies to all of them verbatim: "an A/A null controls within-invocation noise only and is blind to between-worker CPU, cache, and memory-bandwidth differences," demonstrated by a 13.6× same-cell swing with both nulls passing. Payoff: each project learns whether its headline ratios are worker-confounded before quoting them — frankenredis's disavowed table gets a re-baseline doctrine (re-measure with host binding, or retract), and franken_snowflake's unattributed thresholds get the attribution the frankenfs ledger now requires.

**7.2 The Artifact Gates second CI lane → target: asupersync, franken_agent_detection → why it fits them → expected payoff.**
asupersync has "zero workflow runs exist at the pinned commit," so all its behavioral claims rest on "maintainer-run DSR gates, unobservable externally"; a second, observable lane could carry evidence *on the assessed commit*. franken_agent_detection's single lane "dies at `cargo fmt` before a single one of the 1,226 tests executes" — a fmt-independent artifact lane (crash/replay gates, conformance bundles, integrity manifests) would keep the 1,226 tests' evidence flowing while the lint lane is red. Payoff: evidence continuity decoupled from the most fragile gate — the exact decoupling frankenfs already runs at HEAD (Artifact Gates green while main CI red).

**7.3 The transport-confound isolation protocol → target: frankensqlite, frankensearch → why it fits them → expected payoff.**
frankenfs's protocol: symmetric transport arms (loop/loop, file/file, null-device), null-spread gating, and the comparator's existing `--fuse-transport loop` mode — a publishable method for userspace-IO benchmarking, falsifiable on a second FUSE filesystem (the packet's own next-step 4 names ext4fuse). frankensqlite makes storage-I/O and durability-adjacent performance claims on real on-disk formats; frankensearch mandates asupersync for async storage I/O with its own bench culture. Both face the same transport-vs-engine ambiguity frankenfs caught itself in — "a 3.2x swing from transport alone." Payoff: attribute transport vs engine effects *before* quoting numbers, instead of discovering the confound after publication the way frankenfs's own scorecard did.

**7.4 The canonical-gate execution binding (fail-closed `not_implemented`) → target: franken_alignment, franken_engine → why it fits them → expected payoff.**
franken_alignment already uses the `not_implemented` convention for its G1–G6 roadmap gates (in `registry/roadmap.json` [Code-verified, High]) but as *status entries*, not execution bindings — and it carries "~3,482 authored test functions of which ~2,971 have no recorded execution," i.e., the exact population where an empty test selection could masquerade as a pass. FrankenFS's binding — "a cargo filter selecting zero tests is classified `not_implemented`, *never a pass*," pinned by `canonical_gates::tests::catalog_commands_equal_the_spec_document` — is the executable version of franken_alignment's own doctrine. Payoff: kills the "zero tests selected = pass" failure mode inside the suite's most gate-heavy projects; the unqualified-addition disclosure becomes mechanically enforced at the test-selection layer.

**7.5 The merge-proof MVCC taxonomy → target: frankengraphdb → why it fits it → expected payoff.**
frankengraphdb's medium bet B1 is "One Version Universe: MVCC/time-travel/git-style branches/replication as one fountain-coded commit stream ('Chronicle')" — a design bet with no conflict-resolution semantics specified. FrankenFS's `MergeProofMechanism` taxonomy (AppendOnly, RangeOverlay, BitmapOr, BitmapDelta, NoSameBlockMerge) with `ConflictPolicy::{Strict, SafeMerge(default), Adaptive}` expected-loss arbitration is executable merge-without-abort machinery for concurrent same-block writers [Verified, High]. Fit: a fountain-coded commit stream with concurrent writers needs exactly a merge-vs-abort policy, which frankenfs has implemented and frankengraphdb has only sketched. Payoff: turns the "One Version Universe" bet from a design sketch into concrete, testable conflict-arbitration semantics — with frankenfs's own falsification path (a TLA+/Ivy spec of the commit protocol, per its next-step 2) available as the validation template.
