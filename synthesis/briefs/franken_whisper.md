## 3. CI/EVIDENCE BAR

Practices franken_whisper demonstrates that named sibling packets lack, each with the "lacks" evidence:

1. **Formal result-class doctrine with an admissibility gate.** Mechanism: SELF-SPEEDUP = *maintenance* (may justify landing code, never a campaign win); INCUMBENT-WIN / CAMPAIGN WIN requires the actual incumbent binary running side-by-side in the same invocation, incumbent ELF SHA-256 recorded, dual A/A nulls in [0.98, 1.02], WER quality gates. franken_jax **lacks it**: "no A/A null calibration published and no independent rerun" — its genuine AD-overhead advantage "remains a single-worker claim" [Inference, Medium]. franken_snowflake **lacks it**: its statement decoder is "a self-gated threshold on synthetic data with no A/A null". franken_node **lacks the wiring**: "the Criterion-bench controls (pinning, A/A nulls) are not tied to it in the assessed materials — treat the benches as un-audited". Partial-overlap honesty: franken_mermaid has "mandatory A/A nulls and ELF-SHA-256 provenance" and frankensearch has an explicit "SELF-SPEEDUP vs INCUMBENT comparison-class policy" — so the doctrine is an emerging fleet norm; franken_whisper's is the most formal statement of it.
2. **Published non-verdicts as first-class ledger rows** (Metal "NO ADMISSIBLE PERFORMANCE VERDICT"; failed A/A-null campaign "banks NO cross-arm comparison"). In spirit shared with franken_tts ("own negative-evidence ledger killed the flagship speculative optimization — twice, with receipts — and published the autopsy") and franken_networkx (its zero-copy design doc "refutes its own 77,795× headline"), but whisper is the only packet showing the *gate firing in public inside the perf ledger itself* rather than in a retrospective narrative.
3. **`fw doctor` refusing to call itself ready.** Mechanism: a `ready` result requires both ~2.12 GB native model packages to pass static preflight against compiled SHA-256 trust roots — and the payload still sets `operationally_verified: false` until an actual transcription succeeds [Maintainer claim, Medium; trust-root pinning and two-package provisioning flow verified in README and `install.sh`]. franken_markdown (`doctor --json`), franken_ocr (`focr doctor` — verifies source SHA-256 + exact quant recipe without network), and franken_code_browser (`fcb doctor --json`) all have doctor commands but **lack** the refusal semantics and the compiled trust-root gating of model artifacts.
