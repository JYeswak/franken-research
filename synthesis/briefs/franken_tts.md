# Cross-suite brief: franken_tts

Analyst scope: read franken_tts-assessment.md fully; skimmed the other 43 packets (TL;DR, claim inventory, verdict/TRL/NODUS, benchmark sections, limitations, license/governance) and ran targeted term searches across the full packet corpus to verify uniqueness/sharing claims below. Evidence-tier labels are carried verbatim from the packets (e.g. `[Verified, High]`, `[Maintainer claim, Medium]`, `[Inference, Medium]`). Assessment date for all packets: 2026-09-22.

## 1. OWN-PACKET DISTILLATION

- **Verdict:** franken_tts is "a genuinely substantive, shipped, pure-Rust CPU-only runtime for one model — Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-0.6B-Base" [Inference, High], not vaporware: 10 crates, 96,057 lines of first-party Rust [Verified, High], 11 GitHub releases (v0.1.0–v0.1.10, 2026-08-06 → 2026-08-30) with prebuilt binaries for five platform targets plus SHA256SUMS [Verified via releases API, High], a Homebrew tap, an install one-liner, 18 built-in voices, a streaming conversation layer, a native iOS app, and a live WASM playground.
- **TRL:** 7 — "Real releases, installable binaries on 5 platforms, parity receipts, operational surface (robot mode, resident daemon, iOS, WASM) — operational prototype; capped by DISC-003 (listening-eval gap on the default route) and load-dependent RTF."
- **NODUS ring:** Explore — "Pilot requires a release artifact plus a bounded, real workload fit. The release artifacts exist — but the only route fast enough for a real workload is the int8 default, whose quality is not listening-certified (DISC-003 open), while the certified-quality route (f32) runs 6–7× slower than real time and cannot serve one."
- **Strongest strength:** "it actually ships (installable binaries, real receipts, and a maintainer who published his flagship optimizer's two death certificates in-tree)."
- **Strongest ceiling:** "the single-maintainer / no-outside-contributions governance, a non-OSI MIT+OpenAI/Anthropic-rider license that bars exactly the two AI labs building agent-voice infrastructure from even benchmarking it, an open listening-evaluation gap on the default int8 route (DISC-003), and load-dependent real-time performance — with zero independent validation of any kind."

**The 3 most important pieces of evidence (with the packet's tiers):**

1. **The flagship kill, published twice.** "The negative-evidence ledger records the flagship speculative-drafting optimization (FrankenMTP) as dead *twice* — NE-002: sampled-drafter mean acceptance ~0.01 per depth, expected accepted-prefix length 0.04 of 15 frames, 'strictly worse' than sequential; NE-003: greedy-drafter p_token = 0.0000 across 480 proposals, disposition REVERT" [Verified verbatim, High] — while the README's lead "solution" paragraph still markets the dead optimization. This is the packet's central adversarial finding.
2. **The pinned truth pack.** "a pinned truth pack recording the oracle pins (HF weights `5d83992` from 2026-01-29, upstream source `022e286` from 2026-03-17)" [Verified, High], plus manifests, an acceptance surface with break-even thresholds, a nondeterminism floor, and a fetch/verify script — "a portable oracle-capture apparatus, not just fixtures."
3. **The int8 route's measured side effects.** DISC-003 "honestly records that the default int8 route's sampled outputs are 'different valid renditions'... including a measured int8 side effect worth quoting: one enrolled synthetic voice shifted whole-utterance RMS 0.019→0.221 and centroid 860→236 Hz ('audible LF drone risk')" [Verified, High] — the honest row that keeps the ring at Explore.

## 2. SHARED NEGATIVE PATTERNS

Plus: "version badge reads 0.1.9 while `Cargo.toml` is 0.1.10 (stale badge)" and "The README's enrollment section still calls it 'the upcoming higher-quality ICL cloning mode' — stale by one release" [Verified, High].

- asupersync: "zero workflow runs exist at the pinned commit and the most recent runs (2026-09-07, older commits) show failures — the tokio-parity gate failed at its drift-detection step... so every behavioral claim about the assessed tree rests on maintainer-run DSR gates, unobservable externally" [CI-observed, High].

## 3. CI/EVIDENCE BAR

Practices franken_tts demonstrates that the corpus shows other packets lack. (The *ledgers themselves* are fleet-wide — 22 packets carry a negative-evidence ledger — so what follows is not "has a ledger" but the sharper mechanisms. Term searches across all 44 packets found zero hits elsewhere for each item marked unique.)

1. **A standing refusal of inadmissible comparisons — `[NO ADMISSIBLE RATIO]` / OQ-15.** PERF-007 records the model paper's 97 ms first-packet latency as `[NO ADMISSIBLE RATIO]` because the paper measured on "our internal vLLM engine" with no hardware named, and OQ-15 "freez[es] the local CPU oracle environment (torch==2.7.1, librosa==0.11.0, …) and declar[es] 'official CPU is not an admissible G2 performance incumbent and no CPU/GPU ratio may be reported'" [Verified, High]. No other packet has this apparatus (grep: NONE). Nearest kin — franken_whisper's failed A/A-null campaign published as "no admissible verdict" and frankensqlite's refusal of numeric claims — are adjacent honesty moves, not a standing environment-freezing rule.
2. **The full truth-pack: nondeterminism floor + break-even acceptance surface + fetch/verify script.** franken_nlp and franken_ocr both have a "truth pack" (franken_ocr: "a Phase −1 'truth pack' pinning the exact model source commit and fixture hashes... three-pillar release-certification gauntlet"), but `NONDETERMINISM_FLOOR.md`, `ACCEPTANCE_SURFACE.json` (the FrankenMTP break-even thresholds p*≈94–95%, alpha_full*≈40–46%), and `fetch-truth-pack.sh --verify` appear in no other packet's assessment (grep-verified). franken_tts's truth pack is the only one with a measured nondeterminism floor and pre-committed resurrection thresholds.
3. **A retracted ledger entry kept in-tree with its lesson.** NE-006: "This entry was wrong, and the way it was wrong is the useful part" — a 622 MB cold embedding hidden behind a one-axis census, retracted publicly rather than deleted [Verified, High]. No other packet's assessment shows a retracted-in-tree ledger entry (grep-verified).
4. **Machine-implemented skip honesty.** The `summarize_receipts.py` + XFAIL≠SKIP + `require_model!` harness + `GREEN WITH SKIPS` doctrine is grep-unique to franken_tts. Conceptual kin exist — frankenterm's "explicit `skipped_not_proven` qualification gates," franken_ocr's "skip-honest no-weights mode" — but only franken_tts shows the machine path that makes skipping auditable.
5. **Operator-level cross-target divergence debugging (DISC-006).** The browser-vs-CLI divergence was "traced ... to per-target f32 epsilons — input RMSNorm's sum-of-squares/rsqrt diverging first, then Q/K norm+RoPE on identical inputs (libm sin/cos ulps), then SiLU's exp" and resolved under a canonical gate (`FTTS_CANONICAL_NORM=1`): "96,000/96,000 samples, max 0 LSB, tapI/tapM/tapC hashes bit-equal across all 50 frames" [Verified, High] — "byte-exactness as a debugged property across ISAs, not a slogan." franken_ocr and franken_whisper both ship browser/WASM lanes, but neither packet shows this divergence-to-the-operator debugging; the only other "canonical gate" in the corpus (frankenfs) is a different concept (feature-parity gate commands).

What others have that franken_tts should not claim as unique: negative-evidence ledgers (22 packets), discrepancies ledgers (franken_networkx, franken_nlp, franken_node, franken_ocr, franken_whisper, frankenpandas, frankenscipy), CV≤5% timing discipline (frankensympy: "cv≤5% eligibility"; frankenterm: "cv≤5% local Mac timing"), "bit-identical" test language (franken_manim, franken_ocr, frankenpandas, frankengraphdb), and sigstore-signed releases (frankenterm, franken_node) — franken_tts ships binaries with SHA256SUMS only.

## 4. SHARED HURDLES

Structural hurdles franken_tts shares, with evidence:

- **Bus factor 1 + no-merge governance by design.** franken_tts: "bus factor 1; README's 'About Contributions' refuses outside merges" [Verified, High] — "'I do not accept outside contributions for any of my projects… I won't merge them directly.'" Shared with asupersync ("bus factor 1 with outside contributions explicitly refused (README L2620)"), frankenterm ("bus factor 1 by **explicit, deliberate policy**"), frankensqlite ("bus factor 1 with an explicit no-outside-contributions policy"), franken_ocr ("explicitly accepts no outside contributions"), franken_whisper ("Single human contributor: Dicklesworthstone (3,778 commits per API) — bus factor 1"). Term search: ~40 of 43 packets mention bus factor 1.
- **The MIT+OpenAI/Anthropic-rider license.** franken_tts: "MIT License **with an 'ADDITIONAL RIDER / RESTRICTION (OpenAI / Anthropic)'**... *'no rights are granted to any Restricted Party'*... for purposes of the rider, *'use' includes, without limitation: copying, modifying, … benchmarking, testing, analyzing, indexing'"* [Verified verbatim, High]. Near-universal across the fleet (term search: ~35 packets). The rider's own breadth is part of the independent-validation hurdle: franken_tts notes "No third party can publish a benchmark without lawyering the rider first, which helps explain why independent validation is zero" [Inference, Medium]; asupersync: "a non-OSI rider that bars the AI labs from even benchmarking it"; frankenterm: "denies all rights to the two leading AI labs — the precise demographic an agent-orchestration tool needs."
- **Pinned nightly toolchain.** franken_tts: "Rust, edition 2024, pinned `nightly-2026-07-05`" [Verified, High]; its own packet flags "what happens to the tree when the nightly pin ages out?" as an open question. Shared fleet-wide (term search: ~33 packets, e.g. franken_whisper: "366K lines of nightly-only Rust"; frankensqlite: "a pinned dated-nightly toolchain").
- **Velocity vs review depth.** franken_tts: "11 GitHub releases in 24 days (2026-08-06 → 2026-08-30)"; "review depth is one human plus agent assistance, forever"; "The release notes carry 'Generated with Claude Code' markers (observed in the v0.1.7 notes)." Shared shape: frankenpandas "8,621 commits" with "agent co-authorship trailers" counted in-tree (frankenjax likewise: "agent co-authors" trailers, 2,035 closed beads); franken_whisper "3,778 commits" by one human.
- **Load-dependent real-time + the listening gap (franken_tts's distinctive instance of a shared provisional-perf hurdle).** The int8 default is "1.4–1.6× RT on unloaded Mac Mini M4 Pro" but "0.66–1.05×" heavily loaded and "no certified RTF row exists yet"; the WASM lane is "0.31–0.43× real time." The distinctive hurdle others in the model-runtime family do *not* share is the open listening-eval hole on the default route (DISC-003) — "Contract-B listening with a real voice is OPEN."

## 5. GENUINE UNIQUENESS

Only things no other packet does (grep-verified against all 44; the one false positive — franken_drone_geometry_reconstruction's "mosaic" — turned out to be drone orthomosaics, unrelated):

1. **The voice-card mosaic as a robust float-vector channel.** "`ftts card export aria` renders the voice's 1,024-float speaker embedding as a green mosaic — 144×144 cells at two bits per cell, QR-style finder patterns, interleaved Reed–Solomon error correction — and the card survives screenshots and messaging-app recompression" [Maintainer claim + code, High], with "CLI and Swift encoders are bit-identical — proven byte-for-byte against real artifacts and pinned by a CRC test" (CHANGELOG v0.1.6). No other packet has a steganographic vector-transport artifact. The packet itself notes it is "the smallest genuinely novel artifact in the repo, and the one with the clearest path to outliving the model."
2. **The flagship optimization killed twice *by its own ledger while still marketed in the README*.** The ledgers are fleet-wide, but no other packet shows the negative-evidence ledger killing the project's own lead "solution"-paragraph feature (FrankenMTP: sampled ~0.01/depth acceptance; greedy p_token = 0.0000, both REVERT) with the marketing paragraph coexisting unresolved. franken_whisper's 26,846-line ledger and franken_ocr's published losses (hard-page CER tail 0.6165) record failures — but not a flagship kill against live marketing copy.
3. **`NONDETERMINISM_FLOOR.md` + `ACCEPTANCE_SURFACE.json` as pinned truth-pack artifacts** (grep-unique; see §3.2) — a measured nondeterminism floor and pre-committed break-even resurrection thresholds (p*≈94–95% per-depth, alpha_full*≈40–46%) inside the oracle-capture apparatus.
4. **NE-006: the retracted entry kept in-tree** (grep-unique; see §3.3) — a public ledger retraction with the lesson preserved.
5. **`[NO ADMISSIBLE RATIO]` / OQ-15** (grep-unique; see §3.1) — a standing rule that freezes the comparison environment and declares an entire comparison class inadmissible.
6. **DISC-003's listening-eval-shaped hole, instrumented.** No other packet has a *listening* evaluation gap — it is TTS-domain-specific ("an open listening-evaluation gap on the default int8 route (DISC-003)" with the measured "audible LF drone risk": RMS 0.019→0.221, centroid 860→236 Hz). franken_whisper's nearest analog is a WER-pairing caveat on its speed duel, a different measurement domain.

What is merely "also does X" (not unique): negative-evidence/discrepancies/performance ledgers, pinned oracle truth packs (kin: franken_ocr, franken_nlp), CV≤5% timing discipline (kin: frankensympy, frankenterm), skip honesty (kin: frankenterm, franken_ocr), SHA256SUMS-tagged multi-platform releases (kin: franken_ocr, franken_whisper), the asupersync runtime dependency (kin: franken_whisper, frankensqlite, frankensearch), the MIT+OpenAI/Anthropic rider, bus factor 1, pinned nightly, zero independent validation.

## 6. CROSS-POLLINATION IN

Concepts from other packets that should be applied to franken_tts:

1. **PROOF.md proof-bundle discipline → origin: frankenredis.** "Optimization PRs that ship with a SHA-pinned golden RESP transcript, paired/reversed hyperfine runs, and a keep/reject score gate (their `artifacts/optimization/*/PROOF.md`)" — frankenredis's own packet proposes it as "the program's optimization-evidence template." **Why it fits:** franken_tts's optimization history is exactly a trail of pre-committed gates (NE-002/NE-003's FrankenMTP kills; NE-005's int4 microdecoder with two failed speed gates, 0.04× then 0.52× vs shipping int8, recorded as an OPEN OBLIGATION with a pre-committed re-test predicate). **Payoff:** each optimization attempt (int4 routing, any learned drafter) ships a self-contained, re-executable proof bundle instead of prose — the OPEN OBLIGATION becomes machine-checkable.
2. **CI-enforced unsafe-site ledger → origin: franken_lean.** franken_lean runs "a CI-enforced unsafe-site ledger" with "a bidirectional CI census" and "kernel rules anchored to upstream source lines with `expect=` tokens that fail CI on drift." **Why it fits:** franken_tts's memory-safety posture is real but documentary — "5 crates `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`... `ftts-kernels` is the *documented* unsafe island (44 unsafe blocks...)" [Verified, High] — and there is *no in-tree CI at all* to enforce the census (no `.github/` in tree). **Payoff:** the 44-block island and the ftts-ffi C ABI island become CI-enforced invariants; drift between the documented census and the tree fails a gate instead of rotting in comments.
3. **Sigstore/cosign-signed release attestations → origin: frankenterm + franken_node.** frankenterm ships "content-addressed, sigstore-signed release attestation bundles"; franken_node "consumes the incumbent's solution for its own supply chain: cosign keyless signatures (`.sigstore.json`) for binaries" [Code-verified, High]. **Why it fits:** franken_tts has 11 releases × 5 platform binaries secured only by SHA256SUMS, whose whole pipeline runs on off-tree owner infrastructure ("per-run status is unverifiable by any outside party"). **Payoff:** binary provenance becomes independently checkable, partially closing the project's largest evidence gap without needing in-tree CI.
4. **Auto-stamping count script that documents its own drift → origin: frankenterm.** frankenterm's claim-governance includes "an auto-stamping count script that documents its own drift." **Why it fits:** franken_tts's packet enumerates four live drift instances — stale 0.1.9 badge vs 0.1.10 tree, `CI_AND_GATES.md` describing a workflow file that doesn't exist, the `frankentorch-facade` validator guarding a dependency with zero manifest hits, the README's "upcoming ICL cloning mode" one release stale. **Payoff:** the README-vs-code drift that the packet flags as "a governance signal" gets caught mechanically at release time instead of in the next assessment.
5. **Result-class doctrine (SELF-SPEEDUP/MAINTENANCE vs INCUMBENT-WIN) → origin: franken_whisper.** franken_whisper's bench ledger "forbids calling *any* number a 'win' unless the incumbent was in the room" — "a campaign win requires the actual incumbent binary running side-by-side in the same invocation with A/A nulls in [0.98, 1.02]" (doctrine effective 2026-07-27). **Why it fits:** franken_tts's AGENTS.md names Kyutai's 100M model as the "mandatory challenger / ultra-edge second model" with a three-gate bakeoff; its own apparatus only defines when ratios may *not* be reported (OQ-15). **Payoff:** the bakeoff's reporting rules are pre-committed — wins are admissible-by-construction and the bakeoff can't quietly become a self-speedup.

## 7. CROSS-POLLINATION OUT

Concepts from franken_tts that should be exported to named other projects:

1. **Full truth-pack apparatus (pinned oracle + nondeterminism floor + acceptance surface + fetch/verify script) → target: frankenjax.** frankenjax has "861 oracle fixture cases per family" and a "negative-evidence perf ledger recording measured wins and rejections with criterion CIs," but its conformance "rests entirely on maintainer-executed runs" with zero in-tree CI — fixtures, not a portable pinned-oracle apparatus. **Why it fits:** a JAX-parity reimplementation is exactly the shape the truth pack was built for (pinned upstream oracle, reproducible capture flow). **Payoff:** frankenjax's parity claims become re-executable by a third party from pinned oracles instead of maintainer-local fixture runs.
2. **CV≤5% perf-ledger admission gate → target: franken_ocr.** franken_ocr has "a performance ledger that records losing rows" and its own next step proposes stressing the WASM/browser lane with CER-vs-native ratios and wall times ("if CER exceeds the 0.25 aggregate budget or the lane only works on the demo page, it is a demo, not a product"). **Why it fits:** franken_ocr records honestly but does not gate admission — importing franken_tts's CV≤5% admission rule (the gate that rejects the project's *own headline* RTF number) would make those WASM-lane rows admissible-or-rejected before publication. **Payoff:** the browser lane's parity story gets measurement-discipline guarantees instead of narrative honesty alone.
3. **DISC-006's divergence-to-the-operator debugging → target: franken_ocr.** franken_tts traced browser-vs-CLI divergence to per-target f32 epsilons (RMSNorm → RoPE/libm ulps → SiLU exp) and established a canonical gate proving bit-exactness, while ledgering both renditions as valid. **Why it fits:** franken_ocr ships `focr-wasm` + a wasm-int4 browser artifact whose "parity story is the thinnest in the zoo" per its own packet. **Payoff:** franken_ocr's browser/native divergence becomes a debugged property with a canonical gate instead of the corpus the next step has to run blind.
4. **Retraction-in-tree ledger convention (NE-006) → targets: frankenredis, franken_whisper, frankenterm, frankensqlite.** All four run large loss ledgers (frankenredis: 26,485-line `NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE.md`; franken_whisper: 26,846-line ledger; frankenterm: negative-results ledgers; frankensqlite: performance negative-results ledger) with no demonstrated retraction-in-tree instance in their assessments. **Why it fits:** at tens of thousands of lines, wrong entries are inevitable; the NE-006 pattern ("This entry was wrong, and the way it was wrong is the useful part" — kept, not deleted) turns ledger errors into durable institutional knowledge. **Payoff:** ledger credibility compounds — readers learn the failure modes, not just the verdicts.
5. **Voice-card mosaic (robust float-vector image channel) → target: franken_whisper.** The mechanism is domain-general: a 1,024-float vector → self-locating, Reed–Solomon-protected image channel with a pinned bit-identical CRC test across CLI and Swift encoders. **Why it fits:** franken_whisper ships "a Rust-native Sortformer 4-lane speaker diarization port" — a lane that traffics in speaker representations across devices with no portable channel. **Payoff:** speaker vectors get an air-gapped, screenshot-surviving portable form, reusing a mechanism that is already bit-pinned rather than invented fresh.
