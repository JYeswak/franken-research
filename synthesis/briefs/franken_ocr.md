## 1. OWN-PACKET DISTILLATION

- **Verdict:** "a genuinely substantive, unusually self-critical clean-room pure-Rust reimplementation of the Baidu Unlimited-OCR inference stack — not vaporware" [Inference, High].
- **TRL:** 6 — "Tagged releases, trace download uptake (2–81 per binary, 64 crates.io), measured parity receipts — demonstrated in relevant environments; not 7 (no evidence of operational use beyond downloads)" [§4.9].
- **NODUS ring: Pilot.** One of only three Pilot-ringed packets in the entire 44 (the others: asupersync, franken_agent_detection). The mechanical bar cleared: release artifact exists (v0.9.0, six platforms, SHA256 sidecars, installer) plus a bounded real-workload fit (offline CPU document OCR on hosts where the incumbent cannot run). Recorded revisit trigger that rings it back to Explore: dead CI + doc drift + zero independent validation [§4.9].
- **Strongest strength:** "the honesty machinery — measured parity receipts, published losses (including a hard-page CER tail of 0.6165), and disavowals of certificates it doesn't hold" [TL;DR].
- **Strongest ceiling:** "bus factor 1 with an explicit no-contributions policy, zero independent validation of any kind, a non-OSI MIT+OpenAI/Anthropic-rider license that excludes the frontier AI labs from even benchmarking it, and a governance decay signal — the CI workflows were deleted from the tree at HEAD and the last runs (2026-09-03) failed, while the docs still describe CI as the release gate" [TL;DR].

**Three most important pieces of evidence** (packet's tiers preserved):

1. **The engine is real.** Shallow clone of HEAD confirms "105,330 lines of first-party Rust in one library crate" [Verified, High], "a complete wired forward (preprocess → SAM-ViT-B → 16x conv compressor → CLIP-L/14 → projector → 12-layer DeepSeek-V2 MoE decoder with R-SWA → lm_head → sampler)" [Verified module topology, High], and "no `build.rs`; no `tch`/`burn`/`ort`/CUDA in `src` (grep-clean)" [Verified, High]. This is what separates it from plan-stage work.
2. **The fail-closed refusal.** The README states, twice: "The release does not claim the strict three-party OpenPGP certificate," and "the gauntlet finalizer 'exits 1 until fresh CI artifacts and three trusted signers finalize it'" [Verified, High]. A certification machine that refuses to certify what it cannot prove is the packet's signature evidence for the honesty thesis.
3. **The dead CI.** "No `.github/` directory in the tree; the Actions API lists 1,006 historic runs but zero current workflows; the latest runs (2026-09-03, commits `8a685213`/`f3cefd26`) completed **failure** / cancelled. No CI has run in ~3 weeks while commits continued" [Verified, High]. This is the single hardest piece of evidence against the "certified" framing — the certification's CI input no longer exists — and the packet's stated revisit trigger for a ring-down to Explore.

## 3. CI/EVIDENCE BAR: what franken_ocr demonstrates that others lack

Honesty of framing first: the *genre* of franken_ocr's evidence apparatus is **not** unique. Committed negative-evidence ledgers, discrepancies catalogs, performance ledgers, truth packs, skip-honest harnesses, and self-disavowing claims are the FrankenSuite's house style — franken_tts's TL;DR explicitly claims "the most honest evidence machinery found in this program's model-runtime family," with a PIN_RECORD.md truth pack, DISC-001…006, a CV≤5%-gated PERF ledger, the skip-honesty doctrine (`summarize_receipts.py`, XFAIL≠SKIP, `require_model!` harness in `ftts-conformance`) [all Verified, High], and even `ftts robot selftest` proving int8 tiers exactly equal to the scalar reference "on your machine, at this model's real reduction lengths". franken_whisper carries a 26,846-line negative-evidence ledger [Counted, High]. So the bar franken_ocr sets must be stated at the *mechanism* level, not the genre level:

1. **The L0–L5 parity ladder, executed against a real model, with skip-honest no-weights mode.** Mechanism: stage-isolated parity receipts (preprocess cosine 0.99891 → SAM 0.99992 → CLIP 0.99921 → projector/bridge 0.99964 → decoder hidden 0.99995 → lm_head logits 0.99990 → first-token argmax exact, 128818 == 128818, top-5 logit set identical) [Maintainer claim, Medium — "the doc is detailed and stage-isolated via hooked fixtures, but not independently reproduced"]. franken_nlp *plans* an L0–L5 ladder (ASSAY) but at TRL 2 "no path executes end to end" [Maintainer claim, Medium]; franken_tts's closest analog is "seam-by-seam" via truth pack + discrepancies, plus "model-gated ladder skips" in release gates — not a staged numeric ladder. No other packet describes an executed per-stage parity ladder with skip-honest mode.

2. **The three-pillar release-certification gauntlet with fail-closed finalizer.** Mechanism: `scripts/gauntlet_cert.py` + `docs/gauntlet/` — three-pillar scorecard, conformal ratchet (Jeffreys-posterior/Hoeffding lower bounds), Ville e-process invariant monitors, convergence gate (≥10 rounds), capacity certificate (bounded-stream soak, deadlock watchdog); committed `RELEASE_READINESS.json` with 10 blocking cells and per-cell evidence paths; and the finalizer that "exits 1 until fresh CI artifacts and three trusted signers finalize it" [Verified machinery exists, High; gate outcomes Maintainer claim, Medium]. Who lacks it: franken_tts (release gates are "51 test suites and clippy -D warnings green at the tagged commit" + skip audit [Maintainer claim, Medium] — no finalizer, no ratchet), franken_scipy (has a "self-disavowing" gauntlet scorecard but no fail-closed finalizer), franken_term (has "explicit `skipped_not_proven` qualification gates the maintainer refuses to sign off prematurely" — a manual refusal, not a machine finalizer), franken_git (has fail-closed *verification* lanes with exit 3 — same genre, but no release-certification gauntlet). The conformal-ratchet + finalizer combination is franken_ocr's alone.

3. **The code-enforced quant-recipe contract.** Mechanism: `src/quant/convert.rs` test `unlimited_ocr_validated_recipe_has_exactly_2148_int8_tensors` asserts `quantized == 2148` and `names.len() - quantized == 49` ("48 attention + one lm_head") [Verified, High]. franken_tts has int8 tiers and a default-route admission gate; franken_nlp plans "staged immutable quantization recipes … each stage its own parity-gated content-addressed artifact" (unexecuted) [Maintainer claim, Medium]. No other packet describes the exact-tensor-count recipe bound as a code assertion.

4. **The artifact-layer write-time origin binding.** Mechanism: `focr convert` stamps the source safetensors SHA256 into the `.focrq` header (`source_sha256`); `focr pull` verifies every part hash against a committed schema-v2 manifest embedded in the binary [Verified, High]. Who lacks it: franken_whisper verifies against "SHA-256-trust-root model packages" but no packet describes an in-header source-weights stamp; franken_tts's `.fttsq` + SHA-256 side-artifact manifests are the closest cousins; franken_nlp's planned `.fnlpq` "authority freeze" is unexecuted. The packet itself flags the honesty limit: "the write-time origin binding exists but is not tamper-evident end to end" [Inference, Medium].

5. **The fail-closed surface-matrix test.** Mechanism: `docs/FEATURE_PARITY.md` enumerates every surface as `present | partial | missing | n/a | excluded` with "`partial` never rounds up" and `excluded` counted as coverage debt, and `tests/surface_matrix.rs` "fails if a live surface is missing from the ledger" [Verified ledger + test exist, High]. Genre-shared with franken_networkx's "auto-generated ledgers … that fail CI on drift" [Code-verified, High], franken_lean's "`expect=` tokens that fail CI on drift," and franken_graphdb's `claims_lint.toml` — but franken_ocr's is the only *test-level* binding of a feature-parity matrix (others are CI/registry level). Framed honestly: the discipline is suite-standard; the binding point is franken_ocr's.

## 5. GENUINE UNIQUENESS

Only claims that survive the grep cross-check — things no other packet describes:

1. **The disavowed three-party OpenPGP release certificate + fail-closed gauntlet finalizer.** No other packet mentions OpenPGP. The README's double disavowal ("The release does not claim the strict three-party OpenPGP certificate") with a finalizer that "exits 1 until fresh CI artifacts and three trusted signers finalize it" [Verified, High] is franken_ocr's alone. Closest genre-relative: franken_git's dormant verification lanes that "refuse with exit 3 instead of returning a false green" [Verified, High] — same fail-closed *genre*, different object (verification lanes vs. release certificate).
2. **The only OCR engine in the suite — and the only five-model zoo with a music-notation lane.** No other packet mentions OCR engines or TrOMR; the music-lane grep (musicxml|TrOMR) hits only franken_ocr. The zoo (Unlimited-OCR, GOT-OCR2, SmolVLM2, OneChart, Polyphonic-TrOMR), the model-specific int8 kernels with bit-identical scalar oracles, and the custom `.focrq` quantized artifact format are all franken_ocr-only. franken_tts is the one-model counterpoint (Qwen3-TTS-0.6B, single-model depth); franken_whisper is speech-to-text; franken_nlp is plan-stage. The fixed-model *certification* lane is shared with franken_tts, but the OCR workload and the zoo structure are not.
3. **The executed L0–L5 parity ladder with skip-honest no-weights mode** (see §3.1). franken_nlp plans L0–L5 (ASSAY) unexecuted; franken_tts has "model-gated ladder skips" and truth-pack parity but no staged numeric ladder. The per-stage cosine receipts culminating in exact first-token argmax (128818 == 128818) [Maintainer claim, Medium] are the most granular executed parity evidence in the program's model-runtime family.
4. **The three-pillar release certification with conformal ratchet** — Jeffreys-posterior/Hoeffding lower bounds, Ville e-process invariant monitors, convergence gate (≥10 rounds), capacity certificate with bounded-stream soak and deadlock watchdog, committed `RELEASE_READINESS.json` (10 blocking cells, per-cell evidence paths) [Verified machinery exists, High]. Asupersync, franken_engine, franken_graphdb, franken_scipy, franken_sympy, and franken_tui all mention Ville/e-processes, but in *different* contexts (lab runtimes, guardplanes, CASP calibration, intelligence layers) — none in a release-certification gate with a fail-closed finalizer.
5. **The SDOT/SMMLA forced-not-default dispatch with the "NEVER hand-roll wide-SIMD" doctrine.** Hand-written SDOT and SMMLA (i8mm) int8 intrinsics deliberately *not* used by default because "interleaved real-decode measurements showed the LLVM-autovectorized scalar loop beating forced SDOT, with forced SMMLA slower still," so "the runtime dispatches to the autovec path and keeps the intrinsics as forced parity/benchmark sweeps (`FOCR_FORCE_ARCH=sdot|smmla|scalar focr robot selftest`)" [Maintainer claim on the measurements, Medium; dispatch code and the `robot selftest`/`backends` surface verified in the clone, High]. The *measurement-beats-doctrine* genre is shared (franken_tui: "the tree publishes its own losses (SIMD kernels that lose to scalar)"), but the specific forced-vs-autovec dispatch decision and the generalized doctrine ("measured ~5× SLOWER than LLVM autovectorization in the sibling repos") are franken_ocr-only.
6. **The hard-page CER tail as a published limitation with a kill-switch.** Aggregate normalized CER 0.19307925 on the 20-page corpus within the 0.25 budget — but page0590's tail of 0.6164886 published as the honest number, with int4 graduation explicitly gated behind the same kill-switch [Maintainer claim, Medium]. The *publish-losses* genre is suite-standard; the specific tail-risk kill-switch architecture (a single hard page terminating an aggregate-budget pass) is franken_ocr's.
7. **Pilot ring at TRL 6 while the closest sibling rings Explore at TRL 7.** franken_tts is TRL 7 / Explore ("no independent benchmark, review, or production deployment"); franken_ocr is TRL 6 / Pilot — one of only three Pilots in the suite (asupersync, franken_agent_detection, franken_ocr). The ring difference is mechanical (release artifact + bounded real-workload fit), and the packet is explicit that dead CI + drift + zero independent validation are the ring-down triggers — but the *current* Pilot standing is franken_ocr's alone among the model runtimes.

**Deliberately not claimed as unique** (overlap acknowledged): the skip-honest harness mode (shared with franken_tts's XFAIL≠SKIP/`require_model!` doctrine); the truth-pack pattern (shared with franken_tts's PIN_RECORD.md + OQ-15 oracle-env freeze, and planned in franken_nlp); `robot selftest` kernel-vs-oracle proofs (franken_tts has `ftts robot selftest`); the robot/doctor/resident-daemon CLI family (franken_tts ships `robot/doctor/resident-daemon/convert/pull`; franken_whisper has NDJSON robot streaming; franken_markdown has `doctor --json`, `robot-docs`, stable exit codes); the WASM/browser lane (franken_tts has a *live* playground at frankentts.com — franken_ocr's is thinner); six-platform SHA256-sidecar releases + installer + Homebrew tap (asupersync, franken_tts, franken_whisper, franken_sqlite, franken_markdown all ship these); the negative-evidence/discrepancies/perf ledger triad (franken_tts, franken_whisper, franken_networkx, franken_mermaid, and ~25 others).

4. **The conformal ratchet for release decisions → targets: franken_scipy, franken_tui, franken_networkx.** franken_scipy's CASP already does "runtime algorithm selection by Bayesian expected-loss minimization … with audit certificates and conformal-calibration fallback" — the conformal vocabulary is native there; franken_ocr's ratchet (Jeffreys-posterior/Hoeffding lower bounds, Ville e-process monitors, ≥10-round convergence gate) would give CASP's *release* decisions the anytime-valid backing its *runtime* decisions already use. franken_tui already wires e-processes into its intelligence layer — applying them to its release gates is one step. franken_networkx's 726-row ledger with "mandatory A/A nulls" is the raw material a ratchet consumes. **Payoff:** release gates whose statistical claims hold under optional stopping — the failure mode the whole suite's maintainer-hardware numbers are exposed to.

5. **`robot selftest` user-CPU kernel proofs + the frozen-schema-fixture contract test → targets: franken_whisper, franken_libc.** franken_whisper is agent-first (NDJSON robot streaming, TTY audio transport) but ships no user-CPU self-proof of its kernels; a `whisper robot selftest` proving kernel-vs-oracle identity on the *operator's* hardware would extend its honest-loss culture to foreign machines. franken_libc's packet claims "the strongest measurement discipline observed in the program so far" (41,205-line ledger, same-invocation incumbent benchmarks) [Inference, High] — its ceiling is that main CI is red at the pin and numbers are maintainer-machine-bound; user-CPU selftests plus a frozen schema fixture for its measurement receipts would let anyone re-prove the claims where the CI cannot. **Payoff:** evidence that travels to hardware the maintainer never touched — the one property no ledger, however honest, can confer.
