## 1. OWN-PACKET DISTILLATION

**Verdict: TRL 4 · NODUS ring: Explore.** FrankenLibC is a Linux `LD_PRELOAD` interposition layer in Rust (nightly-2026-08-31, edition 2024) reimplementing the C library ABI surface: 4,119 exported symbols individually classified (69.3% native — own Rust or direct syscall; 30.7% validated call-through to host glibc), a policy membrane with strict/hardened modes, an optional 71-kernel runtime-math control plane, and 66 fuzz targets.

**Strongest strength:** the claim/evidence machinery — "the 41,205-line negative-evidence ledger, same-invocation incumbent benchmarks, and published self-retractions are the strongest measurement discipline observed in the program so far" [Inference, High]. The four-field claim contract is the sharp edge: *symbol status is not semantic parity, which is not replacement level, which is not evidence freshness*.

**Strongest ceiling:** "main CI is red at the pin, the shipping artifact is still L1 interposition over host glibc, the bus factor is one, and the MIT+Anthropic/OpenAI rider makes independent evaluation by the best-equipped labs a license violation" [High].

**The 3 most important pieces of evidence (packet tiers preserved):**

1. **Per-symbol classification, machine-checked** — Demonstrated [High]: `support_matrix.json` at the pin counts 4,119 exported symbols — 2,441 `Implemented`, 414 `RawSyscall`, 1,264 `WrapsHostLibc` — yielding the 69.3%/30.7% native/host split. The packet is careful that "native" is the maintainer's taxonomy term, not an independence claim.
2. **Main CI red at the pin** — Disproven (claim 6) [High]: "Pin's main CI run #8942: Core Gates failed on its first step (`scripts/ci.sh`); all substantive jobs skipped. A separate candidate-validation workflow for the pin's change was green, but that is not main CI." Failure root cause not established (CI logs not retrieved — limitation 3).
3. **The silent-deletion self-audit** — Demonstrated [High]: four commits authored 2026-06-26 (committed 2026-08-03, replayed onto the branch so `--since`/`--until` queries miss them) silently deleted shipped work — ~1,300 lines twice over, the entire f128 math engine plus the C23 `fromfp` family (−6,914), the qsort fast/radix lanes. "Three banked benchmark wins stood falsely in the ledger for ten weeks after their code was gone, retracted 2026-09-01." The project caught and published this itself.

**Calibration caveat (cross-suite):** frankenlibc's packet claims "the most rigorously self-audited system this program has assessed" and "the best observed in the program so far" [Inference, High] for the benchmark rules. Sibling packets make directly competing superlative claims: franken_whisper ("the most explicit honesty apparatus observed in this program" [Inference, Medium]); franken_networkx ("the most honest self-scrutiny culture in the FrankenSuite — stronger, on paper, than FrankenRedis's"); franken_node ("the most self-scrutinizing pre-1.0 codebase assessed in this program to date" [Inference, Medium]); franken_lean ("the most self-auditing repo in this assessment program"); franken_engine ("the most methodologically self-policing codebase assessed in this program to date" [Inference, Medium]). The core taxonomy (SELF-SPEEDUP vs INCUMBENT campaign wins, 12 named reward-hacking patterns, negative-evidence ledgers) is demonstrably **suite-shared**, not frankenlibc-originated — see §2. Treat the packet's superlatives as relative-to-packets-completed-at-writing, not measured rankings.

## 3. CI/EVIDENCE BAR — what frankenlibc demonstrates that others lack

Scored strictly against sibling packets; shared items are labeled shared.

**B1 — The silent-deletion / deletion-excess audit (unique — no other packet mentions it).** Method: author-date filter over history (catching commits *replayed* onto the branch so `--since`/`--until` queries miss them), rank candidates by deletions−insertions, adjudicate against the negative-evidence ledger, publish the audit with commit hashes, dates, line counts, and CORRECTION blocks retracting the corrupted claims (three banked wins retracted 2026-09-01). No sibling packet describes any deletion-excess screen. This is the one frankenlibc evidence practice with zero suite peers — and it is the practice that caught the failure mode the other 588 gates missed for ten weeks.

**B2 — Four-field claim separation at per-symbol granularity (unique contract; closest peer noted).** `symbol_status` ≠ `semantic_parity` ≠ `replacement_level` ≠ `freshness_state`, machine-checked across all 4,119 rows of `support_matrix.json` (983 KB). The packet claims "separating these four killed at least three classes of misleading claim in one repo" (Next Step 3) [Inference]. Closest peer: franken_networkx's 4,129/4,129 machine-checked algorithm coverage matrix — comparable per-unit granularity but a *coverage* taxonomy, not a claim-separation contract; its own claim-coverage audit had to do by hand (finding 579/591 KEEP rows unattested) what frankenlibc's contract does by construction. franken_lean's claim matrix has evidence states; franken_engine's 28-claim matrix has wording states; franken_tui's 152-row ledger has proven/retracted/pending states — none separates four dimensions per unit.

**B3 — Executable claim contracts at the largest scale in the suite.** 588 `check_*.sh` gate scripts "encode the claim/evidence contracts in executable form" [Counted, High]. Only one sibling counts gate scripts at all: franken_engine ("292 gate scripts" / "295 gate scripts" in two extractions). franken_tui and franken_networkx each have "five `make gates`" checks; franken_node has `check_*.py` validators (uncounted). 588 is the suite maximum by a wide margin.

**B4 — `dlmopen(LM_ID_NEWLM)`-protected incumbent arm (unique instantiation of a shared doctrine).** The same-invocation incumbent requirement is suite-shared (P7), but frankenlibc's *mechanism* — protecting the host-glibc arm from symbol interposition via `dlmopen` in a new link-map namespace — is specific to its LD_PRELOAD context and appears in no other packet. franken_whisper's equivalent is SHA-256-pinned incumbent ELFs with seven-round A/A nulls; franken_networkx's is ELF-hash pinning in the Gauntlet campaign.

**B5 — Bead-closure rule written from observed violations (unique rule inside a shared system).** The beads issue system is suite-shared (franken_engine: 4,537 records; franken_node: `.beads/issues.jsonl`; franken_tui: "close-audits of beads"), but frankenlibc's rule — "a bead may not close on a gate whose compilation was not observed," written because "four beads closed on gates that could not link ('an unlinkable target is silent, not green')" — appears in no other packet.

**B6 — Largest negative-evidence ledger in the program (size distinction, not kind).** 41,205 lines / 3.39 MB [Counted, High] vs franken_networkx 37,116, franken_whisper 26,846, frankenfs 20,556, franken_search 19,201. The ledger *discipline* is shared (P7); the scale is frankenlibc's.

**Honest non-bars (things frankenlibc does NOT clear that the bar framing must not imply):** its 66 fuzz targets are not the suite maximum (franken_node counts 146, franken_scipy 96) and their CI execution status at the pin is explicitly unverified (packet limitation 8 / open question 8); its benchmark methodology is "best observed" only within its own packet's comparative window (see §1 calibration caveat); its 2.3M-line tree is not the largest (asupersync: 3,864,420 lines of Rust; franken_engine: 3.5M lines).

## 5. GENUINE UNIQUENESS — things no other packet does

**U3 — LD_PRELOAD interposition as the strategy, with WrapsHostLibc staging and L0–L3 replacement levels.** "Interpose over glibc, validate every call's inputs through a policy membrane, reimplement the hottest surfaces natively, call through to the host for the rest" — with the staging model L0 (interpose) → L1 (hardened interpose, current) → L2 (standalone `.so`) → L3 (full replacement) and the explicit 30.7% host-backed disclosure. The strategy's own packet admits its ceiling: "the validator must always run inside the process it distrusts" and "LD_PRELOAD cannot touch static binaries, setuid/setgid binaries, or code that runs before the preload resolves" (DEPLOYMENT.md).

**U4 — The interpose-don't-replace answer to the adoption trap.** Related to U3 but distinct as a *thesis*: frankenlibc is the only packet whose strategy section argues that validation can be decoupled from implementation — "the `WrapsHostLibc` path proves you can validate a call without reimplementing it" (§4.12, decoupling lens). The nearest behavioral peer is franken_whisper, which organically went wrapper → native replacement (`NativeEngineRolloutStage::Sole`) — but as an *execution path that happened*, not as a staged, machine-checked replacement-level taxonomy.

**U5 — Gentoo/Portage per-package operational integration.** `data/gentoo`, `docker/gentoo`, phase allowlists, package blocklists including `sys-libs/glibc` and `sys-apps/shadow` — "the only deployment lane with per-package operational controls." No other packet mentions Gentoo.

**U6 — The bead-closure "compilation observed" rule as an audit-born institution.** Within the suite-shared beads system, frankenlibc alone documents a closure rule written from four observed violations, plus the maintainer's admission that the bead audit sampled 7,422 closed beads for the rule and "predicts [the hit rate] is non-zero."
