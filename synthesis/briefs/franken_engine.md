The matrix emits "exact downgrade text" when wording outruns evidence, and `actual_wording_state == allowed_state` held on all 28 rows at the pin [Counted + Code-verified, High].
- **Strongest ceiling:** "The core CI lane is red at the pin — `native-runtime.yml` run #35550128941 (head = pin) failed all three jobs on test-execution steps, and all 46 recorded runs of the workflow (Sep 16–21) fail — it has never gone green [CI-observed, High]; `quality-gates.yml` and `perf_regression_gate.yml` have **zero runs ever**" — compounded by the license rider that "bars the AI labs (and their agents) that are the likeliest evaluators and adopters of an *agent-extension runtime* from even analyzing the code" [Code-verified (license text), High], bus factor 1, and the headline security contract admitted TARGETED.
- **The 3 most important pieces of evidence (with tiers):**
  1. **The gate bites its own author.** The claim matrix's Performance Evidence section states the rule "plainly — the real hot-path lane counts as observed internal evidence *only* when `scripts/run_real_hot_path_proof.sh smoke` emits `real_runtime_hot_paths` artifacts validated by the contract gate... A project that built the track and still refuses to certify any lap time on it.
  2. **The only unsafe in 3.5M lines is weaponized honesty.** "Zero `unsafe` in library code: `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]` on all lib crates; the only `unsafe` blocks tree-wide are two adversarial negative tests (`transmute`-forged IFC labels)" [Code-verified, High] — at `crates/franken-engine/tests/unsafe_code_prohibition_gate.rs:374` and `crates/franken-extension-host/tests/trusted_label_adversarial.rs:156`, "both do `unsafe { std::mem::transmute(...) }` — forging an IFC `Labeled<String>` — as *negative tests* proving the capability membrane catches label forgery."
  3. **The methodology export is named as the deliverable.** The packet's "Methodology fit (for the FrankenSuite)" reads: "adopt the patterns, not the package — the claim-to-proof matrix with wording-state gating and freshness tiers, the `repro.lock`-partnered verification commands, the no-mock acceptance drills, the self-downgrading performance candor, and the declassification-receipt/transparency-log design are directly importable into how the program evaluates the other 42 repositories" [Inference, High].

## 3. CI/EVIDENCE BAR — what franken_engine demonstrates that others lack

Honest caveat first: the "best honesty machinery" title is *contested* across packets, and the contest is analyst-judgment, not fact. frankenredis claims "Self-scrutiny infrastructure is the best in the FrankenSuite"; franken_networkx claims "the most honest self-scrutiny culture in the FrankenSuite — stronger, on paper, than FrankenRedis's"; frankenlibc claims "the strongest measurement discipline observed in the program so far"; franken_markdown claims "the most rigorous marketing-hygiene mechanism observed in this program." What follows is not the title but the *specific mechanisms* for which no other packet shows evidence:

1. **`repro.lock`-partnered verification commands, per claim.** `docs/claim_to_proof_matrix_v1.json` binds each of the 28 claims to a `repro.lock` partner alongside the owning bead, artifact path, and verification command [Counted + Code-verified, High]. No other packet mentions a per-claim reproducibility partner (franken_ocr's "Phase −1 truth pack" pins model-source commits and fixture hashes — the nearest cousin, but model-level, not per-claim; franken_threed pins an upstream oracle blob SHA-1 for one harness).
2. **Wording-state gating with exact downgrade text.** The gate "refuses README wording whose `actual_wording_state` exceeds its `allowed_state`, emitting exact downgrade text" [Code-verified, High], and `actual_wording_state == allowed_state` held on all 28 rows at the pin. The claim-matrix *family* is shared (franken_lean's B8: "a claim matrix with evidence states and documentation CI that rejects wording stronger than the matrix permits"; franken_markdown's `claims.tsv` + `check-claim-discipline.sh` "wire each README claim to a `capabilities --json` key and a proof script"; franken_tui's 152-row machine-checked claims ledger "56 proven / 56 retracted / 34 pending-doc / 6 pending-code"; franken_node's `check_claims_manifest.py --check-honesty` "9 checks ok, 0 drifted, with live recounts… inside the manifest's tolerance bands" [Verified, High]; franken_git's "machine-checked claims registry (6 verified claim rows)" and "auto-demoting claims registry"). But *no other packet* shows the `actual_wording_state`/`allowed_state` fields, the emitted downgrade text, or a per-claim verification partner — engine is the most executed specimen of the shared family.
3. **Freshness tiers as a decay sensor.** Each claim carries `max_observed_freshness_days` — the packet's governance section notes "the matrix's own freshness tiers — `max_observed_freshness_days` will start auto-downgrading OBSERVED rows faster than re-verification rebuilds them, so the project's honesty machinery becomes its decay sensor, visible on the README itself" [Inference, Medium]. franken_lean's next-steps mention "freshness bounds" as part of its B8 apparatus (so the *concept* is shared with lean), but no other packet shows them wired into the gate's auto-downgrade path at the pin.
4. **CI-enforced fixture rejection as backing evidence.** The Performance Evidence section rule: fixture-only artifacts (`hot_paths_simulation`, `MockCertificate`) are rejected by the gate as backing evidence, and the May 2026 "no-mock acceptance drill (`bd-ly6hp.6`) that rejects fixtures" [Maintainer claim, High]. Many siblings keep negative-evidence ledgers (redis 26,485 lines; libc 41,205; torch 42,708; scipy 44,078; whisper 26,846; pandas 44,086; networkx 37,116; mermaid 726 rows; search 19,201; overlap's `fo-claim-gate`), but none shows a *gate script that refuses fixtures as claim backing* — the ledgers record losses; engine's gate blocks the promotion.
5. **The e-process guardplane as a live containment decision ladder.** `martingale_decision_ledger.rs` implements "a non-negative martingale tracked in fixed-point millionths of log-space… integer-only arithmetic precisely so security evidence never depends on platform `f64::ln`" [Code-verified, High], feeding `eprocess_guardrail.rs` and the six-rung `ESCALATION_LADDER: ["allow","challenge","sandbox","suspend","terminate","quarantine"]` in `agent_sandbox.rs`. e-processes appear elsewhere but never as a *live containment actuation plane*: franken_tui wires them "into resize coalescing, diff strategy, and budget alerting" (rendering heuristics); franken_ocr uses "Ville e-process invariant monitors" in its release-certification gauntlet (release-time, not runtime); asupersync has "e-process oracles for testing" in its `frankenlab` deterministic lab runtime (test-time); franken_sympy has "the e-process/conformal monitoring substrate with no registered live monitor" (unregistered). Engine's is the only demonstrated runtime-containment use.
6. **Signed declassification receipts + `prev_hash`-chained transparency log + MMR inclusion/consistency proofs as a runtime primitive.** `declassification_pipeline.rs`, `transparency_log.rs`, `mmr_proof.rs` — "every evidence entry Ed25519-signed with the originating runtime's key, `prev_hash`-chained; the transparency log carries MMR inclusion/consistency proofs" [Code-verified + Maintainer claim, High]. The packet's Wardley claim: "no incumbent ships 'every authority downgrade is a signed, hash-chained, inclusion-proven event' as a runtime primitive." frankenterm has content-addressed, sigstore-signed release attestation bundles (release-time signing, not per-decision runtime receipts); franken_node's packet mentions transparency logs only via the Sigstore/OpenSSF incumbent comparison ("franken_node's registry signing and evidence ledger are adjacent, not competitive"). Engine's decision-receipt stack is the only runtime-level instance.
7. **The adversarial-negative-test unsafe pattern.** "Every library crate carries `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`… and the sole exceptions are the tests that try to break it" [Code-verified, High]. The `forbid(unsafe)` posture itself is a suite norm (markdown, mermaid, drone, nlp, overlap, agent_detection, threed, node, numpy, pandas, sympy, networkx, and more), but no other packet reports confining *all* unsafe to negative tests that attack the security membrane (`transmute`-forged IFC labels). frankentorch is the negative example of the same norm: "the docs claim 'unsafe forbidden' while the tree holds **116** counted unsafe sites" [Counted, High]; frankensqlite_website markets "0 unsafe blocks / pure safe Rust" against "41 counted unsafe blocks in `fsqlite-vfs`" [Verified, High].
8. **The dependency-postmortem-in-the-manifest doctrine.** The root `Cargo.toml` carries the removed `[patch.crates-io]` block's postmortem as a comment: "a sibling's `fsqlite` from 0.1.18 to a breaking 0.1.19 and took the default build red — with 7 of 16 OBSERVED claims becoming unverifiable," plus the doctrine "no patches, registry-pinned siblings, feature flags that genuinely remove surfaces (`--no-default-features` is a no-siblings build; the Parquet evidence-export path fails closed with a typed error rather than emitting a lesser artifact)" [Code-verified, High]. franken_snowflake has the machine-checked cousin — "a cargo-tree admissibility gate that fails the build if a forbidden crate appears" [Verified, High] — but engine's is the only documented *doctrine-with-postmortem*; snowflake's is gate-without-narrative.

- **Agent-authorship opacity (the hurdle engine notably does NOT share).** frankenjax: "the same labs whose models co-authored 53% of recent commits" [External, High]; franken_torch: "plus agent personas"; frankenpandas: "plus agent co-authors"; franken_threed: "AI-agent-authored at scale with opaque provenance" [Git-observed, High]; frankenredis: "36% agent co-authorship." ## 5. GENUINE UNIQUENESS

Only mechanisms for which no other packet shows any instance. The family-level resemblances are noted and excluded.

1. **A runtime that CI-gates its own marketing with per-claim verification partners, wording-state fields, and emitted downgrade text.** The claim-matrix *family* is shared (lean B8, markdown claims.tsv, tui ledger, git registry, node honesty manifest, sim claim-integrity gating — "also does X"). The *specific instrument* — 28-row JSON with owning beads, `repro.lock` partners, verification commands, `actual_wording_state`/`allowed_state` fields, freshness tiers, and a gate emitting exact downgrade text — appears in no other packet. franken_lean's next-step #1 proposes *porting its own* B8 to a sibling "as a standalone crate"; engine's is the specimen that already runs the fuller form.
2. **The e-process guardplane as live containment actuation** (non-negative martingale in fixed-point millionths of log-space, integer-only arithmetic so "security evidence never depends on platform `f64::ln`," driving the six-rung allow→quarantine ladder). franken_tui's e-processes drive rendering heuristics; franken_ocr's monitor release certification; asupersync's test oracles. Engine's is the only live-decision use — the packet calls it "genesis" and no packet contradicts that.
3. **Signed declassification receipts + prev_hash-chained transparency log + MMR inclusion/consistency proofs as a runtime primitive.** frankenterm's sigstore bundles are release-time; franken_node's receipt talk is in its *unoccupied-lane* analysis, not its code. Engine is the only runtime-level instance.
4. **The IFC lattice as engineered security property** — five labels with manual `Ord` (because "the derived `Ord` would order the `Custom` variant above `TopSecret` by discriminant and break IFC invariants"), join/meet via full `Ord` "so the result is argument-order-independent — commutativity as a security property, with the rationale in comments" [Code-verified, High], default `AllowlistOnly` fail-closed mode, and one-use ciphertext-release receipts "that deliberately carry no plaintext or key bytes" [Code-verified, High]. No other packet mentions IFC at all.
5. **The negative-performance candor *mechanism*.** Self-reported losses are a suite norm (whisper's failed A/A-null campaign published as "no admissible verdict"; fs's published silent-data-loss facade; networkx's self-refuting 77,795× doc; torch's retracted 2× SDPA win). Engine's move is different in kind: the *gate* refuses to promote the Node/Bun denominator while the bundle is dirty, the bundle is preserved *and* declared non-normative, and fixtures are rejected as backing evidence by rule. No other packet shows a performance claim that its own machinery is forbidden to make.
6. **The `[patch.crates-io]` postmortem as a comment block in the root manifest**, with the no-patches doctrine and genuinely-surface-removing feature flags. franken_snowflake's cargo-tree admissibility gate is the machine-checked cousin; engine's postmortem-in-the-manifest is the narrative form, and no other packet shows one.
