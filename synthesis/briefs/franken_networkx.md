## 1. OWN-PACKET DISTILLATION

- **Verdict:** the most methodologically self-honest codebase in the FrankenSuite — 100% machine-checked surface coverage, a 13-variant tie-break engine, a 37,116-line loss ledger — whose own audits show the performance story is 98% self-measured, under a license that forbids the best-equipped evaluators from analyzing it.
- **TRL:** 6 (adjudicated 5-vs-6 in favor of 6: the backend plugin executes live NetworkX workloads inside the incumbent's own pipeline with same-invocation A/A controls, byte-identity checks, ELF pinning; no independent validation and no observed production use block 7) [Inference, Medium on the level; High on the facts].
- **NODUS ring:** Explore [Inference, Medium] — substantive, shipped, unusually self-honest, but unproven.
- **Strongest strength:** the evidence machinery is the artifact — five auto-generated ledgers (coverage matrix, raw-vs-public, delegation, upstream-divergence, API ergonomics) that fail CI on drift [Code-verified, High]; the 4,129/4,129 machine-checked surface result [Counted, High]; a 1,092-file Python parity suite [Counted, High]; a 37,116-line negative-evidence ledger with an enforced INCUMBENT-vs-SELF-SPEEDUP verdict contract [Counted, High]; a Gauntlet head-to-head campaign with same-invocation A/A nulls, byte-identity-before-timing, and ELF-hash pinning [Maintainer claim, High on existence]; and a zero-copy design doc that refutes its own 77,795× headline [Code-verified, High].
- **Strongest ceiling:** the AI-lab license rider (bars OpenAI/Anthropic and affiliates from use, benchmarking, analysis [Code-verified, High]) — an advancement blocker that structurally empties the independent-validation column; compounded by bus factor 1 (single GitHub contributor, 8,808 contributions [External, High]) and no release artifact at the assessed pin (v0.2.2 targets an earlier commit; PyPI serves 0.2.1) [External + Git-observed, High].

**The 3 most important pieces of evidence (tiers preserved):**
1. **The 2.0% claim-coverage audit** — `docs/CLAIM_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md` (2026-07-31, self-authored): *"Of **591** KEEP claim rows across the three active ledgers, **12** carry a vs-incumbent ratio measured with NetworkX live in the same invocation, and **579** do not — **2.0%** attested coverage."* Reproduces via `python3 scripts/perf_ledger_preflight.py --audit` [Code-verified, High]. Be precise about the delta — "also does X" items are marked as such.

**(a) An incumbent-attestation-coverage audit that is *executable at the pin*.** The 2.0% finding is not a static doc: it reproduces via `python3 scripts/perf_ledger_preflight.py --audit` [Code-verified, High], and the same script enforces the INCUMBENT/SELF-SPEEDUP verdict contract as a pre-commit gate. Who lacks it: the other 43 — including frankenpandas, franken_whisper, frankenlibc, frankenscipy, and frankensearch, all of which run the SELF-SPEEDUP/INCUMBENT distinction but none of which evidences a runnable audit of their own attested fraction.
