## 3. CI/EVIDENCE BAR — practices frankenscipy demonstrates that others lack

## 5. GENUINE UNIQUENESS — what no other packet does

- **CASP as a deployed mechanism (not the Bayesian-decision pattern — that is shared).** No other packet mentions CASP or `SolveCertificate` (keyword sweep: 1/44). The *pattern* "strict/hardened dual-mode with Bayesian decision engine" is shared — **franken_numpy**'s `fnp-runtime` runs "Three actions (Allow / FullValidate / FailClosed) selected by a closed-form Bayesian posterior update against a fixed loss model" [Maintainer claim, Medium] and **frankenpandas** has "a strict/hardened Bayesian runtime policy layer (`fp-runtime`)" — so a brief must not claim frankenscipy invented runtime decision-theoretic selection. What is unique is the *instantiation*: calibrated 5×4 `const fn` loss matrices wired across **five** numerical domains with verified `select_action` call sites, a conformal calibrator (`ConformalCalibrator::new(0.05, 200)`) with a drift override (`should_fallback()` at miscoverage > α+0.02), and synchronously returned replayable `SolveCertificate`s asserted present by the conformance harness [Verified wiring, High].
- **The "no SciPy must fail" oracle-absence control job.** Unique among all differential-oracle packets (see §3.1). This is not a method the other 43 numeric projects practice, per keyword sweep [Inference, Medium].
- **G9 tolerance-policy ratchet as a named CI gate.** Unique (see §3.3).
- **The harness-disagreement finding as documented fleet doctrine.** The finding itself is fleet-shared — **frankentorch** adopted it as a standing gate ("name the worker, name the harness, quote both estimators, replicate before quoting — adopted 2026-08-15") [Counted + Maintainer claim, High] — and the measurement originated in **frankenlibc** (5.9459x vs 12.385414x). But frankenscipy's NEGATIVE_EVIDENCE.md (2026-08-15) is the document that records the fleet adoption and frankenscipy's packet is the one that carries it as hook and did-you-know. Uniqueness verdict: frankenscipy is the finding's *canonical home*, not its sole practitioner — say so, don't claim sole ownership.
- **Full-workspace registry publication including the conformance crate.** All 19 crates (conformance included) on crates.io at 0.2.0 [External, High]. Contrast **franken_numpy**: 10 of 11 published, `fnp-conformance` deliberately withheld (404 on crates.io [External, High]); **frankenpandas**: "crates.io publication unverifiable from this network [Not verified]"; **frankenredis**: v0.1.0's 24 signed assets "target an earlier commit, not the pin". The distinguishing detail is the conformance harness being published *as part of* the release — [Inference, Medium] that this is unique in-suite.
- **Inverted uniqueness — no Python surface.** Among the numeric reimplementations frankenscipy stands alone in shipping *no* Python surface: "Can I use this from Python? Not directly today. There is no PyO3 layer." [Verified README L2003–2004, High]. **franken_numpy** exposes "100% of `numpy.__all__` (499/499)" via a PyO3 extension with identity-equal re-exports [Code-verified, High on the mechanism]; **franken_networkx** ships a PyO3 binding crate plus a NetworkX backend-protocol plugin (`backend.py`); **frankenpandas** has maturin wheels building in CI. This is a genuine differentiator — and a ceiling: frankenscipy's stated audience includes agent/edge numerics, but unlike its siblings it cannot meet a Python pipeline where it lives.
- **Not unique (say so):** the negative-evidence ledger (franken_numpy: 67,641 lines; frankenpandas: 44,086; frankentorch: 42,708; franken_networkx: 37,116; frankenredis: 26,485 — frankenscipy's 44,078 lines is mid-pack, not the largest); the self-disavowing scorecard (Pattern C above); zero-unsafe Rust (shared with frankenpandas's all-15-crate-roots forbid census [Counted, High] and ~20 other packets); the discrepancy/divergence ledger (franken_numpy's DIVERGENCES.md with "0 active rows"; franken_networkx's upstream-divergence ledger; frankenpandas's DISCREPANCIES ledger); RaptorQ evidence artifacts (fleet-shared); the strict/hardened mode split (shared with franken_numpy, frankenpandas, and franken_networkx's `CgsePolicyEngine` "enforces strict-vs-hardened parsing with fail-closed defaults" [Code-verified, High]).

CASP is the calibrated generalization: five action sets against `const fn` loss matrices, `ConformalCalibrator::new(0.05, 200)` with drift override to `SVDFallback` at miscoverage > α+0.02, and synchronously returned `SolveCertificate`s enabling replay/determinism checks [Verified wiring, High]. Payoff: replaces their fixed loss models with calibrated matrices + certificates; frankentorch's kernel-selection problem (its per-lane "allocator-gap diagnosis" work) is the third natural host — selection-by-expected-loss with a certificate is exactly the shape of "why did this lane pick this kernel."
2. **The oracle-absence negative control ("no X must fail") → targets: franken_numpy, frankenpandas, frankentorch, frankenredis, franken_networkx → why it fits them → expected payoff.**
   All five run differential conformance against live or vendored oracles (franken_numpy's live-NumPy differential on every CI run [23 differential mentions]; frankenredis's 5,041 probes vs vendored Redis 7.2.4 [Counted, High]; frankenpandas's live-oracle parity gate; frankentorch's PyTorch oracle with 33 fixtures; franken_networkx's vendored NetworkX oracle + 1,092-file parity suite) and none documents an absence control (keyword sweep [Inference, Medium]). Payoff: kills the vacuous-pass failure mode fleet-wide; sharpest where gates are currently red — when franken_numpy's G1–G8 or frankenpandas's live-oracle gate next goes green, the control job is what makes that green *mean* the oracle was exercised.
3. **G8/G9 pair — decode-proof verification + tolerance-policy ratchet → targets: frankenredis, franken_numpy → why it fits them → expected payoff.**
   frankenredis already has the closest analogs on both halves: "SHA-pinned proof bundles per optimization PR" (upgrade target for G8) and a "ratcheted-matrix" scorecard convention (upgrade target for G9's CI-enforced tolerance ratchet). franken_numpy has a G1–G8 topology with a differential harness but no tolerance ratchet, and its pin-day failure was a lint gate — the *policy* gates are where its next failure mode lives. Payoff: tamper-evident parity artifacts with CI-verified decode proofs; a monotone tolerance policy so the tolerance numbers can't silently loosen while the gate stays green.
4. **Canonical parity packets as named release artifacts (report + erasure-code + decode-proof triple) → targets: franken_networkx, frankenredis → why it fits them → expected payoff.**
   frankenscipy ships 18 numbered FSCI-P2C packets as v0.2.0 release artifacts [Verified, High]. franken_networkx already gestures at cross-packet evidence with its "e2e-cross-packet-golden workflow" and its packet's own next step proposes a ComplexityWitness-style receipt "for cross-repo behavioral conformance" [Inference, Medium] — the triple format is the packaging that next step needs. frankenredis's "499 MB / 127,571-file fuzz corpus" and 5,041 differential probes [Counted, High] are evidence that currently lives as in-tree bulk; the triple format makes a probe corpus citable and re-verifiable by a third party without the repo. Payoff: evidence becomes a signed, hash-chained, externally verifiable artifact — the publication format frankenscipy's own next step #4 is trying to invent, proven by a second adopter.

## Analyst notes / caveats

- All cross-packet comparisons derive from packet texts plus keyword sweeps over the 44 assessment files, not re-verification of the repos. "No packet documents X" is an inference over the corpus [Inference, Medium], not a proof of absence in the trees.
- frankenscipy's own packet is the most bullish in the suite on its evidence apparatus ("Evidence discipline without peer in this program" [Verified, High] on the apparatus's existence); sibling packets contest the superlative — frankenredis ("the most rigorously self-audited codebase assessed in this program to date"), franken_networkx ("the most honest self-scrutiny culture in the FrankenSuite"), franken_alignment ("the honesty apparatus is the best in the FrankenSuite"), franken_agent_detection ("evidence discipline... is the best in the program"). Treat "best in suite" claims from all five as comparative judgments over different packet subsets [Inference, Medium]; the convergent fact is that all five carry a negative-evidence apparatus, and frankenscipy's is distinguished by CI-gating the *integrity* of the evidence (G7/G8/G9), which the others do not do.
- frankenscipy's CASP calibration quality remains [Maintainer claim, Medium] — the single load-bearing unvalidated claim for any export of the concept. franken_numpy and frankenpandas should not adopt CASP's *matrices*, only its *machinery*, until calibration is independently validated.
