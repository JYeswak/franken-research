## 4. SHARED HURDLES

The destroy-on-breach variant (shared with frankenjax, frankenmermaid, frankensim) is harsher than frankenredis's. The frankenpandas-specific irony: the rider bars the AI labs and agent frameworks that its own §4.6 demand-side analysis names as the two most plausible buyers of its evidence machinery ("the target user is excluded by name" [Inference, Medium]).

**H3 — README/ledger drift vs. audit cadence.** Frankenpandas documents the drift itself (four README spots, two DISC entries [Code-verified, High]) and the mitigation is the same everywhere: beads-tracked reality checks. The unmeasured variable the packet names — "the drift rate vs. audit rate is unmeasured" [Inference, Medium] — is true of the whole suite; frankenmermaid's 3x line-count staleness and franken_markdown_website's version drift are the same disease without the self-measurement.

**H4 — Velocity vs. review depth.** Extreme velocity everywhere: frankenpandas 8,621 commits [External, High]; frankenredis ~43/day (Aug) decelerating to ~16/day [Git-observed, High]; franken_threed 380 commits in 13 days; franken_node ~20/day; franken_code_browser 101 commits on one day. Review depth is parked as an open question in *every* packet I skimmed (frankenpandas §4.13 Q7: "Whether any of the 4,049 closed beads saw a second pair of eyes" [Not verified]).

**H5 — Zero independent validation.** Frankenpandas: "A web search for independent benchmarks, reviews, or production users of frankenpandas returned nothing beyond the repository" [External, High within recall caveats]. Same finding in frankenredis, frankentorch, franken_node, frankensqlite, franken_whisper, frankenterm. The rider's "analyzing/benchmarking" bar makes this *structurally* self-reinforcing: the labs that could validate may not legally evaluate — frankenpandas's §4.7.1 names it the binding constraint: "A project whose moat is *evidence* forbidding evidence-gathering is self-negating" [Inference, High].

**H6 — Pinned-nightly toolchain / reproducibility burden.** Frankenpandas pins a nightly toolchain with `portable_simd` and a 2024-edition feature, and its own reproduction note demands a single pinned host or target-cpu pinning [Counted/Code-verified, Medium]. Cousins: frankenmermaid ("on a pinned nightly"), frankensqlite ("pinned dated-nightly toolchain"), franken_whisper ("366K lines of nightly-only Rust"). This compounds the build-variance finding (M3): reproducibility requires pinning both the toolchain *and* the machine.
