# Hurdles and Issues

Structural blockers to adoption, collaboration, and evaluation, with the maintainer's stated intent set against the observed outcomes. Conflicts are stated, not smoothed. Tiers: [Verified], [CI-observed], [Maintainer claim], [External], [Inference] + High/Medium/Low.

## H1 — The license rider: intent vs observed cost

**What it is.** 38/44 repos carry a non-OSI MIT+OpenAI/Anthropic rider [Verified, High]. Exact scope (frankensim LICENSE, quoted verbatim at the pin): Restricted Parties are "OpenAI, L.L.C.; Anthropic, PBC; any of their respective Affiliates; and any person or entity acting directly or indirectly on behalf of, for the benefit of, or under the direction of any of the foregoing (including any officer, director, employee, contractor, agent, consultant, service provider, or representative)." "Use" is defined to include "copying, modifying, … benchmarking, testing, analyzing, indexing, or incorporating the Software or any Derivative Works into any dataset, training corpus, evaluation harness, or pipeline." Breach = automatic immediate termination with a duty to "immediately cease all use and distribution… and destroy all copies under your control"; injunctive relief and prevailing-party attorneys' fees stipulated; the rider controls over the MIT text in any conflict. [Verified, High]

**Intent (from the maintainer's writing).** The rider was added after plain MIT: no license → plain MIT on 2026-01-21 → rider on 2026-02-21, then enforced suite-wide [Maintainer claim, High]. No direct maintainer rationale or legal-enforceability argument for the rider was found in the writing surveyed. That the intent is anti-training-data is [Inference, Medium] — plausible, but not a maintainer statement. His broader commercial signals: consulting for PE/hedge funds, a paid SaaS skills marketplace, a premium prompt library, and disclosed spend of ~$13.5K/month on 63 agent subscriptions [Maintainer claim, High]. No fundraise, hiring page, team, acquisition, or FrankenSuite business model was found; the self-funded consulting/SaaS reading is [Inference, Medium].

**Observed costs.**

1. **It bars the likeliest evaluators.** Benchmarking, testing, and analyzing are defined as "use," so the two frontier AI labs cannot independently validate any rider-bearing repo without lawyering the rider first (franken_tts's packet makes this explicit) [Verified, High] — whether the labs would attempt validation absent the rider is the untested causal step [Inference, Medium]. For an agent-infrastructure program, the rider excludes the precise demographic the projects need [Inference, High].
2. **It contradicts the agent-first thesis.** `frankensim`'s architecture is explicitly agent-first (agentic simulation, agent-run V&V, bead/bv workflows for agent operators); `franken_tts` targets agent-voice infrastructure; `franken_node`'s threat model is written for agent operators. The rider bars the labs building the frontier agents from touching them [Verified, High]. The packet's own verdict: the "agent-first" thesis is self-refuting without a benchmark/analysis carve-out [Inference, High].
3. **It sits in tension with the program's own production method.** The maintainer's writing frames AI development as: human as architect/orchestrator, plan and execution separated, multi-model dialectical critique, state externalized into plans, agents implementing, evidence machinery standing in for line-by-line review [Maintainer claim, High]. The commit record shows Claude-family co-authorship at scale (frankenredis 162/449, frankenpandas 398, frankenjax 52/100, frankentorch ~45%) [External, High] — i.e., the Restricted Parties' models are inside the production process while the license bars those same parties from the output. Whether any agent co-author counts as "acting for" a Restricted Party is untested [Inference, Medium] — needs counsel, not an analyst (the commit record itself is [External, High]).
4. **Enforceability is unverified.** No court test, no legal memo in the corpus; "requires counsel" is the honest status [Inference, High]. Some packets mis-disclose the rider itself: `franken_engine`'s README badge said "License: MIT" while LICENSE carried the rider; `franken_manim`'s `Cargo.toml` declares `license = "MIT"` while LICENSE is MIT-with-rider; `franken_native_capsule` ships no license text at all [Verified, High]. The program that gates honesty has no gate on its own license disclosure — except `frankengit`, whose `license_gate.sh` + AGENTS.md mandate is the sole machine-enforced disclosure apparatus [Verified, High].

## H2 — The collaboration ceiling

Bus factor 1 in 44/44 [Verified, High]. Explicit refusal of outside contributions in 19/44 [Maintainer claim, High]. No succession plan, no second committer, no foundation anywhere in the corpus. Velocity makes this acute rather than theoretical: franken_engine (96 commits in a day), franken_code_browser (101 in a day), frankensim (999 commits in five days; 6,545 contributions in ~11 weeks), frankentorch (6,305 commits), frankenpandas (8,621 commits) [External, High]. Review depth is one human plus agent assistance, permanently [Verified, High].

The maintainer's writing is explicit that this is *by design*: agents implement, the human orchestrates, and the evidence machinery stands in for line-by-line review [Maintainer claim, High]. The observed cost: the machinery that is supposed to substitute for review is itself unaudited by any second party, and in 42/44 cases it is not even executing in public CI at the pin. The frankensim packet measured the consequence directly: 202 test files outpaced the project's own honesty inventory in 11 hours — development velocity exceeds audit velocity [Verified, High]. The collaboration ceiling is therefore not just "one person": it is one person whose audit automation cannot be inspected, at a velocity no successor could inherit. If the maintainer stops, each project freezes mid-sprint with no qualified cut to fork from [Inference, High].

## H3 — The evaluation paradox

The maintainer's 2024 alignment writing argues internal guardrails should not be trusted and demands external hard evidence; the "radical transparency" philosophy predates the suite [Maintainer claim, High], and the continuity with the repositories' evidence machinery is real [Inference, High]. But the suite's negative evidence is almost entirely **self-published**: self-run gates, self-graded claim matrices, self-retained receipts [Verified, High]. Independent third-party validation is 0/44 [External, High]; analyst-executed behavioral reproduction is 1/44 (`franken_threed`, 61/61 re-run by the analyst in ~21 s) [Verified, High].

This is the paradox, stated plainly: the program's epistemology demands external evidence, and the program's license (H1) and governance (H2) structurally prevent external evidence from existing. The rider bars the best-equipped validators; the no-contributions policy bars the rest; the private CI (DSR/RCH) makes even the maintainer's own evidence unobservable in 6/44 cases. The honesty apparatus is genuine — ledgers of killed findings, retained failures, self-disqualifications are all real artifacts [Verified, High] — but an audit that only the audited party can run is a diary, not an audit [Inference, High]. The frankentui packet's line stands as the program's epitaph-in-advance: "a ledger that only the maintainer's private DSR pipeline can verify is a diary, not an audit."

## H4 — Evidence debt at the pin

42/44 projects cannot demonstrate public CI green at the assessed commit (11 red, 6 no pin verdict, 11 no test CI, 8 disabled/deleted, 6 private-only). [CI-observed, High] The failure shape that recurs: lint/format gates (fmt, clippy) fail first and every substantive gate behind them is skipped — so "red CI" usually means *unexecuted*, not *refuted* [Inference, High]. The five sharpest mechanism-without-execution instances: `franken_lean`'s unsafe census passing inside a red gate; `franken_markdown`'s claim-discipline gate living on DSR [Maintainer claim, High] while the in-tree workflow is disabled; `franken_engine`'s quality/perf workflows with zero runs; `franken_manim`'s merge-blocking self-goldens with pin greenness unestablished; `frankengit`'s auto-demoting registry whose enforcement loop is maintainer-local. [CI-observed, High] Full matrix: `ci-requirements.md`.

The maintainer's writing prizes shipping rapidly and building in the open [Maintainer claim, High]. The observed cost of shipping rapidly without green CI is a program where the evidence machinery is the most developed artifact and the least executed one.

## H5 — Release discipline

29/44 projects have no release at the pin: 23 with no release or tag at all, 5 whose release artifacts target an earlier commit (`frankenredis`, `franken_networkx`, `franken_engine`, `frankenpandas`, `franken_node`), and 1 phantom (`frankentui`'s v0.9.0 — CHANGELOG-described, no tag, no GitHub Release, no crates.io package) [External, High]. The earlier-commit pattern is the subtle one: signed assets and installers exist, but they certify a tree that is not the assessed one. Named in `negative-patterns.md` P6.

The maintainer ships constantly (21 GitHub releases for `frankenterm`, 11 in 24 days for `franken_tts`, 20 for `asupersync`) [External, High] — release *activity* is high where it exists; release *coverage of the assessed commit* is the gap.

## H6 — What the maintainer's writing does not resolve

- **No FrankenSuite endgame was found.** The closest statements: "Building the AI Infrastructure of the future"; building agent tools, research, protocol infrastructure, shipping rapidly [Maintainer claim, High]. No interview or podcast explaining the suite endgame was found; X posts were not systematically reviewed.
- **No suite-level "not building" statement.** Only project-level non-claims (e.g. frankensim: "no general CAD/CAE application or crates.io release is claimed") [Maintainer claim, High].
- **Comprehension of the generated corpus is unresolved.** The stated method (evidence machinery substituting for line-by-line review) is [Maintainer claim, High]; whether anyone — maintainer included — comprehends millions of generated lines is unanswered [Inference, High].
- **Co-maintainers and funding are unverified.** The writing shows consulting income and SaaS experiments, not a team or a fundraise [Inference, Medium].

## The net

The suite's genuine asset is a transferable honesty methodology (contracts with no-claim boundaries, negative-evidence ledgers, claim-integrity gates, fail-closed lanes). Its structural liabilities are the license rider, the collaboration ceiling, and the evaluation paradox — and all three are *governance* choices, not engineering deficits. No amount of correct engineering inside the trees climbs them; only a rider carve-out, a second committer, and publicly executing CI would. Those are the revisit triggers that matter. [Inference, High]
