## Exact corrections and adjudications

It does **not** impose a blanket ban on non-restricted evaluators. Suggested replacement: “The rider withholds rights from OpenAI, Anthropic, their affiliates, and persons acting for, benefiting, or under their direction; its definition of use includes benchmarking and ML evaluation pipelines. It does not bar a non-restricted evaluator merely for using those labs' models. This narrows access by Restricted Parties; the mission/audience cost is [Inference, Medium].” Keep Explore-with-a-ceiling if its scope is the restricted-party limit, not a universal ban.
