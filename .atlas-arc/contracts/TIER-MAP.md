# TIER-MAP: rigor-atlas and franken-harvest labels onto the Rulebook's tiers (UNK-007 proposal)

Status: PROPOSED by ContractArchitect, 2026-09-24; revised 2026-09-25 after review 5 (Luna, SIGN-WITH-CORRECTIONS): principle 4 now names its detector and failure codes, principle 6 splits every Tier-1 basis into what was checked and what was not, TM-RA-1 is conditional on a build-time citation check (new TM-RA-10 catches the rest), and §4.1 states the raw-to-normalized fh state table. Every rule here has a gate sub-check, a fixture and a failure code in `gate-matrix.tsv`; the fixtures are in `fixtures/tier-map.json`. UNK-007 is BLOCKS_BUILD for the rigor-atlas and fh ingest beads; its resolution requires an independent reviewer to sign this mapping (unknowns.jsonl row UNK-007). Until signed, no `ra:` or `fh:` entry may ship.

## 1. The target vocabulary (RULEBOOK.md §1)

- Five tiers, RULEBOOK.md:27-33: 1 `[Verified]` (direct inspection of a fresh clone, an API response or a live page by the analyst), 2 `[CI-observed]`, 3 `[Maintainer claim]` (asserted in README/docs, not independently executed), 4 `[External]`, 5 `[Inference]` (the analyst's judgment).
- Flavors, RULEBOOK.md:35-42: a flavor such as `[Code-verified]` is allowed only if it maps to exactly one tier and the legend states the mapping. This document is that legend for search results.
- Confidence, RULEBOOK.md:44: High (multiple converging sources or direct inspection), Medium (single solid source), Low (thin evidence).
- Claim statuses, RULEBOOK.md:74: demonstrated, partially demonstrated, aspirational, disproven, stale.
- Independence, RULEBOOK.md:53: "A second agent report is a cross-check, not independence."
- Every claim needs a tier and a grade, RULEBOOK.md:19.

## 2. Principles

1. **Never upgrade.** When a source label could map to two tiers, take the weaker one (the generic rule "demotions are always allowed").
2. **Tier follows the evidence the row cites, not the label alone.** A KNOW row that cites only a README is the maintainer's claim about the code, whatever the label says.
3. **No rigor-atlas or fh claim is graded High.** Both were produced by agents (rigor-atlas: `agent-A01..A16` and `agent-B01..B07`; intake-IntakeRigor2 §3) and Franken Research has not re-graded them. That is one source, a cross-check at best (RULEBOOK.md:53), so Medium is the ceiling (`Q3.HON-11.TM_CONFIDENCE_HIGH`, fixture TMF-10).
4. **Anything unmapped fails the build.** An unknown label, an empty label, or a mixed free-text label never falls through to a default tier. Each table below lists its closed label set: techniques `KNOW`, `INFER`, `GUESS`; prescriptions `KNOW`, `INFER`; profile `kind_epistemic` and `invariant_epistemic` `KNOW`, `INFER`, `GUESS`, plus the mixed free-text form TM-RA-7 names for profiles only; fh raw states exactly as §4.1 spells them, case-sensitive. The detector is `tm_unmapped_label` (gate Q3, sub-check HON-11). It runs before any rule is chosen and prints one line per offending row, `FAIL Q3.HON-11.TM_UNMAPPED_LABEL detector=tm_unmapped_label reason=<unknown|empty|mixed> system=<rigor-atlas|franken-harvest> row=<snapshot key> label=<the raw label, JSON-quoted>`. `reason=empty` is an absent, empty or whitespace-only label; `reason=mixed` is a label that contains two or more closed-set labels or any text beyond one (for example `KNOW for the installer … INFER for the spec` on a technique, where TM-RA-7's profile handling does not apply); `reason=unknown` is anything else (`BLIND`, `know`, `VERIFIED`). fh raw states use the same detector with code `Q3.HON-11.FH_STATE_UNMAPPED`. Fixtures TMF-01 to TMF-04 and FHN-09, FHN-10.
5. Every mapped entry records `evidence.mapped_from = {system, label, rule}` so a reviewer can audit the conversion row by row. `label` is the raw source label or raw checker state, before normalization. A tiered `ra:` or `fh:` entry without it fails (`Q3.HON-11.TM_MAPPED_FROM_MISSING`, fixture TMF-11).
6. **A Tier-1 basis says what was checked and what was not, in that order, and nothing more.** Tier 1 here means one thing: a build-time check found the cited text at the cited lines at the pinned commit (§4.1). It never means the interpretation was checked; that stays the atlas agent's reading (rigor-atlas) or franken-harvest's judgment (fh). So for rules TM-RA-1, TM-FH-1 and TM-FH-2 `evidence.basis` is not free text. The generator renders it from the snapshot row with the template for its rule, and gate Q3 re-renders it and requires byte equality:
   - TM-RA-1: `Checked at build: the cited lines are at {path}:{lines} in {repo}@{commit7}. Not checked: what they show is a rigor-atlas agent's reading (label KNOW), not re-graded by Franken Research (TIER-MAP TM-RA-1).`
   - TM-FH-1: `Checked at build: the quoted lines are at {path}:{lines} in {repo}@{commit7}. Not checked: that they are an example of {fh_id} is franken-harvest's judgment (TIER-MAP TM-FH-1).`
   - TM-FH-2: `Checked at build: the quoted lines are at {path}:{lines} in {repo}@{commit7}, moved from {pinned_lines}. Not checked: that they are an example of {fh_id} is franken-harvest's judgment (TIER-MAP TM-FH-2).`

   `{path}`, `{lines}` and `{repo}` must equal `evidence.source.path`, `evidence.source.lines` and `evidence.source.repo`, and `{lines}` must be the lines the check actually found. A basis that differs from its rendering fails with `Q3.HON-11.TM_BASIS_TEMPLATE_MISMATCH`; when the difference is in the `Checked at build:` clause and that clause asserts more than presence (it matches `\b(prove[sd]?|shows? that|demonstrat\w*|correct\w*|guarantee\w*|implements?|verified that|is an? (example|instance) of)\b`, case-insensitive), the code is `Q3.HON-11.TM_BASIS_OVERCLAIM`; when `{lines}` names lines the check did not find, `Q3.HON-11.TM_BASIS_ARTIFACT_MISMATCH`. No other rule may produce Tier 1. When no build-time check ran or it did not normalize to `CURRENT`, the entry cannot be Tier 1: TM-RA-10 and TM-FH-3 to TM-FH-5 apply. Fixtures TMF-05 to TMF-09 (TMF-00 is the clean control). The card renders the two clauses as two lines, so the badge `[Verified]` sits next to "Checked at build" and not next to the practice's claim.

## 4. franken-harvest (`fh:`)

The catalogs carry no epistemic label. What they carry is a citation (`repository`, `revision`, `path`, line range, verbatim quote), and fh's own verifier classifies a citation with four states (`franken-harvest/src/verify.rs:48-53` at `77d515b`: `CURRENT`, `PINNED_UNVERIFIED`, `STALE`, `CANNOT_DETERMINE`) plus a `line_relocated` flag (`verify.rs:102`). fh states the limit of that check itself: "citation agreement does not prove row prose, interpretation, completeness, or causal conclusion" (`src/cli.rs:2488` at `77d515b`; line 2381 in the dirty working tree). Our snapshot check (IF-SOURCES.md §4.2 step 4) does not run fh's verifier. It reproduces the check with its own vocabulary, which differs: `search/lib/quote-check.mjs` emits `CURRENT`, `RELOCATED` (with the number of places found and their line ranges), `STALE`, `NO_REVISION` and `EMPTY_QUOTE`. §4.1 maps both vocabularies onto the rule column below; the rules read only normalized states.

The raw state is written to `evidence.mapped_from.label` (principle 5), so a reviewer sees `RELOCATED` on a TM-FH-2 entry. Gate Q3 re-runs the normalization over the snapshot's recorded raw states and fails with `Q3.HON-11.TM_RULE_MISMATCH` when an entry's `mapped_from.rule`, tier, label or confidence differs from what the table and §3/§4 give. **Why the checker was fixed rather than the table (review 5b).** The first version of our checker (a scratch Python script, since ported to `search/lib/quote-check.mjs`) tested `quote in text` with no guard, so an empty quote came back `CURRENT`, and it recorded `RELOCATED` as a bare boolean with no count, so N1 and N5 could not be decided from its output. Making the table follow that checker would have written two upgrades into the contract (a Tier-1 badge on a row that quotes nothing, and a copy target at a guessed place when the quote occurs twice), against principle 1. So the checker was fixed on 2026-09-25: it emits `EMPTY_QUOTE` before any substring test, and for `RELOCATED` it records `occurrences` and every `found_at` line range, which the snapshot row keeps. ## 5. Where the mapping is lossy

1. **Whose inspection.** Tier 1 means the analyst inspected a fresh clone (RULEBOOK.md:29). For TM-RA-1 and TM-FH-1/2 our inspection is the build-time citation check (the lines are at the pin), nothing more; the reading of those lines is one atlas agent's or fh's, and Franken Research has not repeated it. Principle 6 makes the basis say exactly that, and the confidence cap (Medium) says it again. A reviewer who still reads `[Verified]` as "the practice is verified" should push TM-RA-1 to Tier 5 (the rule then equals TM-RA-10).
2. **Not fresh.** The atlas pins are from 2026-09-02 and 111 of 138 repos have moved since (intake-IntakeRigor2 §8); fh pins are older. The tier describes the pinned commit, not today. Cards must show the pin date (UNK-009).
3. **Docs as the product.** TM-RA-2 grades a docs-only KNOW as a maintainer claim. For a repository whose document is the artifact (acip ships a prompt specification), the atlas agent's reading was direct inspection of the product, and the mapping under-grades it.
4. **Path heuristic.** Docs-only versus code is a filename regex. A `.md` file that is the formal specification, or a code file that is only a stub, lands on the wrong side. The error runs toward under-grading KNOW rows and over-grading GUESS rows (TM-RA-5 still lands on Tier 5).
5. **Profiles lose KNOW.** A profile's invariant marked KNOW becomes Tier 5 under TM-RA-7, because the profile table has no line-level evidence for it.
6. **Advice versus exemplars.** A prescription's KNOW describes its exemplars; the advice itself is a synthesis and is Tier 5 whatever the label.
7. **One grade per entry.** RULEBOOK.md:44 grades each claim. An entry shows one grade for its headline claim; the grades of individual exemplars live on the copy targets.
8. **fh citation versus claim.** TM-FH-1 grades the citation, which fh itself says proves nothing about interpretation (cli.rs:2488). Principle 6 fixes the basis wording so a reader of a rigor-layer card sees "Checked at build: the quoted lines are at …" and "Not checked: that they are an example of … is franken-harvest's judgment" as two separate lines.
9. **Line relocation.** TM-FH-2 cites the relocated lines; the original pinned range survives only inside the basis (`moved from …`) and in the snapshot row.
